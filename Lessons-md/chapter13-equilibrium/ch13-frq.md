> 📌 **Scope — one CED exclusion applies**
>
> Covers **CED 7.1–7.2** (§13.1), **7.3** and **7.5**
> (§13.2, §13.5), **7.4** (§13.2–13.4), **7.6**
> (§13.3–13.4), **7.7** (§13.6), **7.8** (§13.1) and
> **7.9–7.10** (§13.7). Equilibrium sits in CED Unit 7 and is one of
> the most heavily tested ideas on the exam.
> 
> The CED exclusion on topic 7.3 removes **converting between $K_c$
> and $K_p$**. Zumdahl derives $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$ in §13.3; that
> conversion is not assessed and is not asked for here. Writing and using
> $K_p$ directly from partial pressures remains examinable.
