> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> Every trend explanation must use nuclear charge, distance, or
> shielding. “It wants a full shell” and “it is more stable” earn zero on
> the AP exam, so they earn zero here.

**1.** Rank each set and give the Coulombic reason:

1. Atomic radius, largest first: Li, Na, K
   
2. Atomic radius, largest first: Na, Mg, Al
   
3. First ionization energy, largest first: F, O, N
   

**2.** Explain each period-2 anomaly:

1. Why is $I_1$ of boron less than that of beryllium?
   
2. Why is $I_1$ of oxygen less than that of nitrogen?
   

**3.** Successive ionization energies (kJ/mol). Identify the group and
justify from the data:

1. 496, 4562, 6910, 9543 $\to$
   group 1 — jump after $I_1$
2. 786, 1577, 3232, 4356, 16,091 $\to$
   group 14 — jump after $I_4$
3. 578, 1817, 2745, 11,577 $\to$
   group 13 — jump after $I_3$ (Al)

**4.** Explain why $I_2$ is always greater than $I_1$, regardless of
element. 

**5.** Photoelectron spectroscopy. A spectrum shows peaks at 273, 26.8,
20.2, 2.44, and 1.25 MJ/mol with relative areas $2:2:6:2:5$.

1. Write the electron configuration.
   $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^5$
2. Identify the element, citing your evidence.
   Cl — areas total 17 electrons
3. Which peak corresponds to the electrons removed first on
   ionization? 1.25 MJ/mol (3$p$)
4. Why does the 2$p$ peak (20.2) lie at lower binding energy than the
   2$s$ peak (26.8), given both are in shell 2?
   

**6.** **FRQ (10 points).** Answer all parts.

1. The complete PES spectrum of an element shows peaks at 208, 18.7,
   13.5, 1.95, and 1.06 MJ/mol with relative areas $2:2:6:2:3$.
   Determine the electron configuration and identify the element.
   
2. Explain what the *position* and the *area* of a PES peak
   each represent. 
3. Predict two specific ways sulfur's spectrum would differ from this
   one. 
4. The 1$s$ peak sits at 208 MJ/mol while the 3$p$ peak sits at 1.06
   — a factor of nearly 200. Explain this enormous gap.
   
5. Successive ionization energies for this element are 1012, 1907,
   2914, 4964, 6274, and 21,267 kJ/mol. Explain how these data
   independently confirm your identification in part (a).
