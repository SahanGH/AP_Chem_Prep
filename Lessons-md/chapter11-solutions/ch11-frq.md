> 📌 **Scope — most of this chapter is excluded**
>
> Only §11.1–11.3 are examinable, as **CED 3.7** (solutions and
> mixtures) and **3.10** (solubility). Read this before assigning:
> 
> The CED exclusion on topic 3.8 removes **colligative properties
> entirely**, and with them **calculations of molality, percent by
> mass and percent by volume**. That takes §11.4–11.8 — vapour-pressure
> lowering, boiling-point elevation, freezing-point depression, osmotic
> pressure, the van't Hoff factor and colloids — off the exam completely.
> None of it appears below.
> 
> Concentration on the AP exam means **molarity**. Every calculation
> here uses it.
