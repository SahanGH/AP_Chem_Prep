referring to its structure.

**4.** `CED 8.1` Conjugate strength.

1. The stronger the acid, the weaker its
   conjugate base.
2. $K_a \times K_b$ equals $K_w$

   acetate. $5.6\times10^{-10}$

   NH₄+. $5.6\times10^{-10}$
5. Rank as bases, weakest first: Cl-, F-, CN-.
