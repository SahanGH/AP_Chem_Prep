> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> Add reactant $\to$ right • add product $\to$ left
> • compress $\to$ fewer moles of gas • inert gas at
> constant $V$ $\to$ no shift • catalyst $\to$ no shift
> • temperature is the only change that alters $K$.

**1.** `CED 7.9` For N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g),
$\Delta H = -92$ kJ, state the direction of shift and whether $K$
changes:

1. Add N₂ right; $K$ unchanged
2. Remove NH₃ right; $K$ unchanged
3. Compress the vessel right; $K$ unchanged
4. Add argon at constant volume
   no shift; $K$ unchanged
5. Add a catalyst no shift; $K$ unchanged
6. Raise the temperature left; $K$ decreases

**2.** `CED 7.9` Explain, in terms of partial pressures, why adding an
inert gas at constant volume causes no shift.

**3.** `CED 7.9` Explain why compressing the vessel shifts
N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ to the right.

**4.** `CED 7.9` Temperature.

1. For an exothermic forward reaction, heat behaves as a:
   product
2. Raising the temperature therefore shifts it:
   left
3. And $K$ therefore: decreases
4. For an endothermic forward reaction, raising the temperature does
   what to $K$? increases it
5. Why is temperature the only disturbance that changes $K$?
   

**5.** `CED 7.10` $K = 4.5$ for H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI. For each
mixture calculate $Q$ and state the direction of change:

1. $[\text{H₂}] = [\text{I₂}] = [\text{HI}] = 0.50$ M
   $Q = 1.0$; shifts right
2. $[\text{H₂}] = 0.40$, $[\text{I₂}] = 0.30$, $[\text{HI}] = 0.40$ M
   *(working space)*
3. $[\text{H₂}] = [\text{I₂}] = 0.10$, $[\text{HI}] = 0.30$ M
   $Q = 9.0$; shifts left
4. $[\text{H₂}] = 0.20$, $[\text{I₂}] = 0.20$, $[\text{HI}] = 0.424$ M
   $Q = 4.5$; already at equilibrium

**6.** `CED 7.10` A system at equilibrium has extra product added.
Explain the resulting shift using $Q$ rather than Le Ch\^atelier.

**7.** `CED 7.10` When is comparing $Q$ with $K$ the right tool and Le

**8.** `CED 7.8` Particulate representations. A sealed flask is drawn
at three successive times. The first two pictures differ; the second and
third are identical.

1. What can you conclude?
   equilibrium was reached by the second picture
2. What must be true of the total number of atoms of each element
   across all three? it is conserved
3. If a diagram shows 8 product particles and 2 reactant particles for
   A ⇌ B, estimate $K$.
   about 4
4. Why is “the reaction stopped” the wrong description of the last
   two pictures? 

**9.** `CED 7.9` For 2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g),

tension.
