> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> $K = [\text{products}]^{\text{coef}}/[\text{reactants}]^{\text{coef}}$,
> omitting pure solids and liquids • $Q$ has the same form at any
> moment • $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}$,
> $R = 0.08206\,\mathrm{L\cdot atm/mol/K}$.

**1.** `CED 7.1` Describe what is happening at equilibrium at the
particle level, and state precisely what is equal.

**2.** `CED 7.1` Starting from pure reactants:

1. Initially the forward rate is: at its maximum
2. Initially the reverse rate is: zero
3. As the reaction proceeds, the forward rate
   falls and the reverse rate
   rises.
4. Equilibrium is reached when: the two rates
   become equal

**3.** `CED 7.1` A student looking at a concentration–time graph says

concentrations are equal at equilibrium.” Identify both errors.

**4.** `CED 7.3` Write the $K$ expression for each:

1. N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g)
   $[\text{NH₃}]^2/([\text{N₂}][\text{H₂}]^3)$
2. 2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g)
   $[\text{SO₃}]^2/([\text{SO₂}]^2[\text{O₂}])$
3. CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g)
   $[\text{CO₂}]$
4. Fe₃O₄(s) + 4H₂(g) ⇌ 3Fe(s) + 4H₂O(g)
   $[\text{H₂O}]^4/[\text{H₂}]^4$
5. 2H₂O(l) ⇌ 2H₂(g) + O₂(g)
   $[\text{H₂}]^2[\text{O₂}]$
