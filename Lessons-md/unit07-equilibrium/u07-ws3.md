> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> Set up Initial / Change / Equilibrium, drive the change row from
> the coefficients, then substitute into $K$ • approximate only
> when justified, and always report the percentage.

**1.** `CED 7.7` ICE tables and the BCA tables from Unit 4 have the
same shape. State the one difference and why it arises.

**2.** `CED 7.7` A ⇌ B + C with
$K = 1.0\times10^{-5}$ and $[\text{A}]_0 = 0.100\,\mathrm{M}$.

1. Complete the equilibrium row: $[\text{A}] =$
   $0.100 - x$, $[\text{B}] = [\text{C}] =$
   $x$
2. Write the $K$ expression in terms of $x$.
   $x^2/(0.100 - x)$
3. Apply the small-$x$ approximation and solve.
   *(working space)*
4. Carry out the 5% check explicitly.
   
5. Give the three equilibrium concentrations.
   $[\text{A}] = 0.099$; $[\text{B}] = [\text{C}] =         1.0\times10^{-3}$

**3.** `CED 7.7` A ⇌ 2B with
$K = 4.0\times10^{-6}$ and $[\text{A}]_0 = 0.50\,\mathrm{M}$.

1. Why is the change in B written as $+2x$ rather than $+x$?
   
2. Solve for $[\text{B}]$ at equilibrium. 
   *(working space)*
3. Check the approximation. $0.14\%$ — valid

**4.** `CED 7.7` The same setup as problem 2 — A ⇌ B + C
with $[\text{A}]_0 = 0.100$ — but now $K = 0.20$.

1. Apply the small-$x$ approximation and report the value it gives.
   $x = 0.141$
2. Carry out the check. What does it show?
   
3. Solve properly with the quadratic. 
   *(working space)*
4. By what percentage was the approximation wrong?
   about 93%

**5.** `CED 7.7` For each case, predict whether the small-$x$
approximation will hold, and say why:

1. $K = 1\times10^{-8}$, $[\text{A}]_0 = 1.0$ M
   valid — $K$ is minute beside $[\text{A}]_0$
2. $K = 2.0$, $[\text{A}]_0 = 0.10$ M
   invalid — $K$ exceeds $[\text{A}]_0$
3. $K = 1\times10^{-6}$, $[\text{A}]_0 = 1\times10^{-5}$ M
   

**6.** `CED 7.7` H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI with $K = 50.0$ and both
reactants starting at 1.00 M.

1. Write $K$ in terms of $x$ and explain why no quadratic formula is
   needed. 
2. Solve for $x$. 
   *(working space)*
3. Give the three equilibrium concentrations.
   $[\text{H₂}] = [\text{I₂}] = 0.220$;
   $[\text{HI}] = 1.56$
4. Verify by substituting back into $K$. 
   *(working space)*

**7.** `CED 7.7` Solve A ⇌ 2B with $K = 0.50$ and
$[\text{A}]_0 = 1.00\,\mathrm{M}$ using the quadratic formula.

*(working space)*

**8.** `CED 7.7` Why is a negative root always discarded when solving

> 📌 **Spiral review • Unit 7 • blocks 1–3**
>
> 1. Reversing a reaction gives $K =$ $1/K$
> 2. Doubling coefficients gives $K =$ $K^2$
> 3. $K \gg 1$ favors: products
> 4. $Q > K$ means the system shifts:
>    left
> 5. Only what changes $K$? temperature
