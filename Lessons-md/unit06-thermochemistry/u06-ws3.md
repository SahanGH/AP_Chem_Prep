> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> $\Delta H$ scales with the coefficients and flips sign when the
> reaction is reversed • enthalpy is a state function, so
> reactions and their $\Delta H$ values add.

**1.** `CED 6.6` For
CH₄(g) + 2O₂(g) → CO₂(g) + 2H₂O(l), $\Delta H = -890.5$ kJ.

1. Energy released per mole of CH₄:
   890.5 kJ
2. Energy released by 0.500 mol of CH₄:
   445.3 kJ
3. $\Delta H$ for 2CH₄ + 4O₂ → 2CO₂ + 4H₂O(l):
   $-1781$ kJ
4. $\Delta H$ for CO₂(g) + 2H₂O(l) → CH₄(g) + 2O₂(g):
   $+890.5$ kJ
5. Which of those three manipulations changed the *sign*, and
   which changed the *magnitude*?
   

**2.** `CED 6.6` For 2CO(g) + O₂(g) → 2CO₂(g),
$\Delta H = -566$ kJ.

1. Energy released per mole of CO:
   283 kJ
2. $\Delta H$ for 2CO₂(g) → 2CO(g) + O₂(g):
   $+566$ kJ
3. $\Delta H$ for CO(g) + tfrac{1}{2}O₂(g) → CO₂(g):
   $-283$ kJ
