> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> When testing a mechanism, always run both checks: do the steps sum
> to the overall equation, and does the rate-determining step reproduce the
> experimental rate law?

**1.** Write the rate law for each elementary step:

1. N₂O → N₂ + O: $k[\text{N₂O}]$
2. NO + O₃ → NO₂ + O₂:
   $k[\text{NO}][\text{O₃}]$
3. 2 NOCl → 2 NO + Cl₂:
   $k[\text{NOCl}]^2$
4. Why is it legitimate to use coefficients as exponents here, but not
   for an overall equation? 

**2.** The overall reaction NO₂ + CO → NO + CO₂ has the
experimental rate law rate $= k[\text{NO₂}]^2$. Consider the mechanism:

$$ \begin{align*}   \text{NO₂ + NO₂ & → NO₃ + NO} &&\text{slow}\\   \text{NO₃ + CO & → NO₂ + CO₂} &&\text{fast} \end{align*} $$

1. Show that the steps sum to the overall equation.
   
2. Identify the intermediate. NO₃
3. Show that the mechanism predicts the observed rate law.
   
4. Explain why CO does not appear in the rate law even though it
   is a reactant. 

**3.** Explain Zumdahl's funnel analogy for the rate-determining step.

**4.** Collision model and energy:

1. State the two requirements for a collision to produce reaction.
   
2. A reaction is highly exothermic but extremely slow at room
   temperature. Explain how both can be true.
   
3. Explain why a modest temperature increase can multiply the rate
   several-fold. 

**5.** Catalysis:

1. How does a catalyst increase a reaction rate?
   
2. Name three things a catalyst does NOT change.
   $\Delta H$, equilibrium position, products
3. In this mechanism, classify each species:
   Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂; ClO + O → Cl + O₂.
   
4. Give the rule that distinguishes them.
   

**6.** **FRQ (10 points).** The gas-phase reaction
2 NO(g) + O₂(g) → 2 NO₂(g) was studied at 25 °C.

| Trial | $[\text{NO}]$ (M) | $[\text{O₂}]$ (M) | initial rate (M/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.010 | 0.010 | $2.5\times10^{-5}$ |
| 2 | 0.020 | 0.010 | $1.0\times10^{-4}$ |
| 3 | 0.010 | 0.020 | $5.0\times10^{-5}$ |

1. Determine the order with respect to each reactant, showing the
   trials compared. 
2. Write the rate law and calculate $k$ with units.
   *(working space)*
3. Predict the initial rate when $[\text{NO}] = 0.030\,\mathrm{M}$ and
   $[\text{O₂}] = 0.015\,\mathrm{M}$.
   *(working space)*
4. The following mechanism is proposed:
   $$ \begin{align*}           \text{NO + NO & → N₂O₂} &&\text{fast equilibrium}\\           \text{N₂O₂ + O₂ & → 2 NO₂} &&\text{slow}         \end{align*} $$
   Identify the intermediate and verify that the steps sum to the
   overall equation. 
5. Show that this mechanism is consistent with your rate law from (b).
