> The unit exam's FRQ is this shape and length. Write everything
> first; the rubric is printed after the last part. Score like a reader: no

**1.** **Long FRQ (10 points).** Answer all parts. Nitrogen and
oxygen form several species; this question examines NO₂- and
NO₂+.

1. Give the total valence electron count for each species.
   
2. Draw a complete Lewis diagram for NO₂+. 
   *(working space)*
3. Draw BOTH resonance forms of NO₂-. 
   *(working space)*
4. Predict the O–N–O bond angle in each species and justify the
   difference. 
5. In NO₂-, the two N–O bonds are equal in length. Explain using
   your diagrams from (c). 
6. Predict which species has the shorter N–O bonds, and justify with
   bond order. 

> 📌 **Rubric — score yourself, 10 points**
>
> **(a) 1 pt** both counts correct, charge handled both directions.
> **(b) 2 pts** 16 e$^-$ structure: 1 for correct connectivity/multiple
> bonds, 1 for lone pairs + charge bracket.
> **(c) 2 pts** both forms, lone pair on N, 18 e$^-$ each.
> **(d) 2 pts** 1 for each angle WITH domain-count justification; bare
> angles earn 1 max.
> **(e) 1 pt** hybrid/average language with equal order — “flipping
> between forms” earns 0.
> **(f) 2 pts** 1 for NO₂+, 1 for the order comparison $2 > 3/2$.
