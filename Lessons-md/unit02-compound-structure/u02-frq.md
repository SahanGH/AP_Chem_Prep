>
> Covers `CED 2.1` through `CED 2.7`, worth

> unit-cell geometry is enrichment and is not asked. Topic `CED 2.7`
> excludes **hybridization involving $d$ orbitals** — for more than
> four electron domains only the *shape* is required, never an
> sp³d label — and also excludes **molecular orbital theory**.

> 
> Every structural explanation must reach the **Coulombic** level.
> Rubrics award zero for “it wants a full octet”.
