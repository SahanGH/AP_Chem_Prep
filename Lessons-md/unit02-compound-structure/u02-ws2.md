**2.** Compute formal charges for the sulfate skeleton drawn with
four single bonds (32 e$^-$):

1. FC on S: $+2$    FC on each O:
   $-1$
2. Verify the sum against the ion's charge.
   $+2 + 4(-1) = -2$ ✓

**3.** Cyanide, CN- (10 e$^-$), could be drawn C$\equiv$N with
the lone pair on C or on N shifted variants. The accepted structure puts
$FC(\text{C}) = -1$, $FC(\text{N}) = 0$. A student objects: “negative charge
should sit on the MORE electronegative atom — this structure is wrong.”
Respond.
