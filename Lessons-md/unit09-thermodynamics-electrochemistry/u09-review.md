## First: which two of the three are you moving between?

| **$\Delta H^\circ,\ \Delta S^\circ \to \Delta G^\circ$** | $\Delta G^\circ = \Delta H^\circ - T\Delta S^\circ$. Convert
  $\Delta S^\circ$ from J/K to kJ/K first |
|---|---|
| **$\Delta G^\circ \leftrightarrow K$** | $\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K$, $R$ in J/(mol$\cdot$K). Qualitative on the
  MCQ half |
| **$\Delta G^\circ \leftrightarrow E^\circ$** | $\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ$, $F = 96485\,\mathrm{C/mol}$, $n$
  from the *balanced overall* equation |
| **Away from standard** | $\Delta G = \Delta G^\circ + RT\ln Q$. Compare $Q$ with $K$; no Nernst
  equation is required |

> 
$\Delta G^\circ  1 \iff E^\circ_{\text{cell}} > 0$   

 One fact, three languages. Every conversion question is translation.

## Entropy, in one block

- **Count the gas moles first.** If that number changes, it
   decides the sign and nothing else matters.
