**9.** **FRQ (10 points).** `CED 9.8` `CED 9.9` `CED 9.10`

1.0 M Zn(NO₃)₂ and a silver electrode in 1.0 M
AgNO₃, joined by a salt bridge.

1. Identify the anode and the cathode, and state the direction of
   electron flow through the external wire.

   *(working space)*

   *(working space)*

   the effect on $E_{\text{cell}}$ and justify without calculating.

> 📌 **Rubric — score yourself, 10 points**
>
> **(a) 2 pts** 1 for both electrodes correctly assigned, 1 for electron
> direction stated as anode $\to$ cathode • **(b) 2 pts**
> 1 balanced equation with Ag+ doubled, 1 for $n = 2$ — an unbalanced
> equation earns 0 for both • **(c) 1 pt** $+1.56$ V; using
> $2(0.80)$ earns 0 • **(d) 2 pts** 1 correct substitution

> joules as kilojoules earns 0 for that point • **(e) 2 pts**

> required.
