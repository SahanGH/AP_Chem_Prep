**3.** Complete the table relating molar solubility $s$ to $K_{sp}$:

1. MX (e.g. AgCl): ions are $s$ and $s$, so $K_{sp} =$
   $s^2$
2. MX₂ (e.g. PbI₂): ions are $s$ and $2s$, so $K_{sp} =$
   $(s)(2s)^2 = 4s^3$
3. M₂X (e.g. Ag₂CrO₄): ions are $2s$ and $s$, so
   $K_{sp} =$ $(2s)^2(s) = 4s^3$
4. M₃X (e.g. Ag₃PO₄): $K_{sp} =$
   $(3s)^3(s) = 27s^4$
