> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> Write oxidation states above the symbols before you conclude
> anything. For stoichiometry, convert volume $\times$ molarity to moles as
> the very first step.

**1.** Assign the oxidation state of the named element:

1. Mn in MnO₂: $+4$
2. Cr in Cr₂O₃: $+3$
3. S in SO₃²⁻: $+4$
4. N in NH₄+: $-3$
5. Cl in ClO₂-: $+3$
6. O in H₂O₂: $-1$

**2.** For each reaction: is it redox? If so, name what is oxidized,
what is reduced, and both agents.

1. Cu(s) + 2 AgNO₃(aq) → Cu(NO₃)₂(aq) + 2 Ag(s)
   
2. BaCl₂(aq) + Na₂SO₄(aq) → BaSO₄(s) + 2 NaCl(aq)
   
3. CH₄(g) + 2 O₂(g) → CO₂(g) + 2 H₂O(g)
   

**3.** A student writes: “Zinc is oxidized, so zinc is the oxidizing
agent.” Correct the statement and explain the naming convention.

**4.** Solution stoichiometry:

1. Moles of Ba²⁺ in 35.0 mL of
   0.200 M BaCl₂:
   7.00e-3 mol
2. Mass of BaSO₄ ($M = 233.39$) if that all precipitates:
   1.63 g
3. Volume of 0.150 M Na₂SO₄ needed to react with it
   exactly: 46.7 mL

**5.** 40.0 mL of 0.100 M AgNO₃ is
mixed with 40.0 mL of 0.150 M NaCl.

1. Moles of each reactant:
   Ag+ 4.00e-3 mol; Cl-
   6.00e-3 mol
2. Limiting reactant, with reasoning: 
   *(working space)*
3. Mass of AgCl formed ($M = 143.32$):
   0.573 g
4. Moles of excess reactant remaining:
   2.00e-3 mol Cl-

**6.** Titration calculations:

1. 25.0 mL of HCl needs 28.6 mL
   of 0.125 M NaOH. Find $[\text{HCl}]$.
   0.143 M
2. 20.0 mL of 0.200 M H₂SO₄ is
   titrated with 0.250 M KOH. Volume needed?
   *(working space)*
