**1.** **Long FRQ (10 points).** A student investigates the
reaction between aqueous lead(II) nitrate and aqueous potassium iodide. She
mixes 50.0 mL of 0.100 M Pb(NO₃)₂ with
50.0 mL of 0.100 M KI and observes a bright
yellow solid.

1. Write the balanced formula equation, including states.
   
2. Write the net ionic equation and identify the spectator ions.
   
3. Determine the limiting reactant, showing the comparison that
   justifies your answer.
   *(working space)*
4. Calculate the mass of PbI₂ ($M = 461.0\,\mathrm{g/mol}$)
   produced. 
5. In the box, draw a particulate representation of the solution
   *after* the reaction is complete. Show at least six particles,
   label your symbols, and omit water.
   *(working space)*
6. The student claims the yellow colour alone proves a chemical
   reaction occurred. Evaluate this claim.
   

> 📌 **Rubric — score yourself, 10 points**
>
> **(a) 1 pt** balanced equation with correct states •\
> **(b) 2 pts** 1 net ionic with the 2:1 coefficient, 1 both spectators
> • **(c) 2 pts** 1 both mole values, 1 comparison that uses
> the 1:2 ratio and names I- — comparing raw moles earns 0 •\
> **(d) 2 pts** 1 applies the 2:1 ratio to get 2.50e-3 mol, 1 mass with
> units • **(e) 2 pts** 1 precipitate drawn as a cluster with
> spectators separated, 1 leftover Pb²⁺ shown •\
> **(f) 1 pt** identifies precipitate formation as the stronger
> evidence.

**2.** Short redox practice. For
2 KMnO₄ + 5 H₂C₂O₄ + 3 H₂SO₄ → 2 MnSO₄ + 10 CO₂ + K₂SO₄ + 8 H₂O:

1. Oxidation state of Mn on each side:
   $+7 \to +2$
2. Oxidation state of C in H₂C₂O₄ and in CO₂:
   $+3 \to +4$
3. Oxidizing agent and reducing agent:
   KMnO₄ oxidizing; H₂C₂O₄ reducing
