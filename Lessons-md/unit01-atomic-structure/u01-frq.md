> 📌 **Scope — two CED exclusions apply**
>
> Covers `CED 1.1` through `CED 1.8`. Unit 1 is worth
> 7 %–9 % of the exam.
> 
> Topic `CED 1.5` excludes **assigning quantum numbers to individual
> electrons**, and `CED 1.7` excludes **electron configurations of the
> aufbau exceptions** (Cr, Cu). Neither is asked below.
> 
> Trend explanations must be **Coulombic** — nuclear charge,
> distance, shielding. The rubrics award zero for answers resting on
> “stability” or “wants a full octet”.

### Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

A photoelectron spectrum of an unknown element from period 3 shows four
peaks with relative heights $2$, $2$, $6$, $2$ and a fifth of height $1$,
listed from highest binding energy to lowest.

1. Identify the element and write its complete electron
   configuration.
   *(working space)*
2. State which peak lies at the highest binding energy and justify it
   in Coulombic terms.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
3. The first ionization energies of magnesium and aluminium are 738
   and 578 kJ/mol respectively. Magnesium's is
   *higher*, even though aluminium has the greater nuclear
   charge. Explain.
   *(working space)*
4. State the ion aluminium forms and give the formula of the compound
   it forms with oxygen.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

### Free-response 3 • short • 4 points
