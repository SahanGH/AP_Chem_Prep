> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> Follow the three-step order every time: Lewis structure $\to$
> VSEPR domains $\to$ hybridization. Show the domain count with every
> hybridization answer.

**1.** Explain, in your own words, why hybridization is proposed at all.
Use methane as the example. 

**2.** Give the domain count and hybridization of the underlined atom:

1. C in CCl₄: 4 domains, sp$^3$
2. N in NH₃: 4 domains, sp$^3$
3. C in CO₂: 2 domains, sp
4. B in BCl₃: 3 domains, sp$^2$
5. O in H₂O: 4 domains, sp$^3$
6. S in SO₂: 3 domains, sp$^2$

**3.** A student assigns sp hybridization to the oxygen in water,
reasoning that oxygen forms two bonds. Identify the error and give the
correct answer. 

**4.** Distinguish $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds:

1. How do the orbitals overlap in each?
   
2. Which type is formed from unhybridized $p$ orbitals?
   $\pi$
3. Which type permits free rotation? $\sigma$
4. Why is the first bond between two atoms always $\sigma$?
   

**5.** Count the $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds in each molecule:

1. CH₄: 4 $\sigma$, 0 $\pi$
2. H₂CO: 3 $\sigma$, 1 $\pi$
3. C₂H₂: 3 $\sigma$, 2 $\pi$
4. CO₂: 2 $\sigma$, 2 $\pi$
5. N₂: 1 $\sigma$, 2 $\pi$

**6.** Restricted rotation.

1. Explain why rotation about a C=C double bond is blocked but
   rotation about a C–C single bond is free.
   
2. C₂H₂Cl₂ with one Cl on each carbon exists as two separable
   compounds when the carbons are joined by a double bond, but not
   when joined by a single bond. Explain.
   

**7.** For acrylonitrile, H₂C=CH-C#N:

1. Hybridization of each carbon, left to right:
   sp$^2$, sp$^2$, sp
2. Total $\sigma$ bonds: 6
3. Total $\pi$ bonds: 3
4. Approximate H–C–H angle:
   $\approx120^\circ$
