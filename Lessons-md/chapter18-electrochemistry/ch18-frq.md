> 📌 **Scope**
>
> Covers **CED 9.8** (galvanic and electrolytic cells, §18.1,
> §18.7), **9.9** (cell potential and free energy,
> §18.2–18.3), **9.10** (nonstandard conditions, §18.4) and
> **9.11** (electrolysis and Faraday's law, §18.7). §18.5–18.6
> (batteries, corrosion) are applications and are not separately assessed.
> 
> The CED exclusion on topic 9.8 concerns the **$+$/$-$ labelling**
> of electrodes, which differs between cell types and is not assessed.
> Anode and cathode are assessed, and the rule never changes: oxidation
> occurs at the anode in *both* cell types.
> 
> $F = 96485\,\mathrm{C/mol}$. Standard reduction potentials:
> Cu²⁺/Cu $= +0.34$ V, Zn²⁺/Zn $= -0.76$ V,
> Ag+/Ag $= +0.80$ V.
