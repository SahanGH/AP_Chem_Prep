### Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

For 2NO(g) + O₂(g) → 2NO₂(g), the following initial rates were
measured at constant temperature.

| **Experiment** | $[\text{NO}]_0$ (M) | $[\text{O₂}]_0$ (M) | **Initial rate** (M/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.020 | 0.010 | $2.5\times10^{-5}$ |
| 2 | 0.040 | 0.010 | $1.0\times10^{-4}$ |
| 3 | 0.020 | 0.020 | $5.0\times10^{-5}$ |

1. Determine the order with respect to each reactant, showing your
   reasoning.
   *(working space)*
2. Write the rate law and state the overall order.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
3. Calculate $k$, with units. 
   *(working space)*
4. Explain why the order with respect to NO cannot be assumed
   from its coefficient of 2 in the balanced equation.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

### Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

1. Identify the intermediates and state how you recognized them.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
2. Write the rate law this mechanism predicts, with justification.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
3. Describe how the energy profile for this three-step mechanism
   differs from that of a single-step reaction, and state which
   barrier is highest.
   \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
4. A catalyst is introduced. State its effect on the rate, on
   $\Delta H$, and on the position of any equilibrium.
   *(working space)*
