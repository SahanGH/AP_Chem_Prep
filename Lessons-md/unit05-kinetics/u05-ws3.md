> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> Elementary step: coefficients *are* orders •\
> $k = Ae^{-E_a/RT}$ •\
> $\ln(k_2/k_1) = -\tfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\tfrac{1}{T_2}-\tfrac{1}{T_1}\right)$
> • $R = 8.314\,\mathrm{J/mol/K}$ •\
> $E_a(\text{fwd}) - E_a(\text{rev}) = \Delta H$.

**1.** `CED 5.4` Write the rate law for each *elementary* step:

1. A → products rate $= k[\text{A}]$
2. A + B → products
   rate $= k[\text{A}][\text{B}]$
3. 2A → products rate $= k[\text{A}]^2$
4. 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂ as a single step
   rate $= k[\text{NO}]^2[\text{O₂}]$
5. Why is (d) an unlikely elementary step?
   

**2.** `CED 5.4` Explain precisely why coefficients give orders for an
elementary step but not for an overall equation.

**3.** `CED 5.7`–`CED 5.8` Overall: 2NO₂ + F₂ → 2NO₂F,
experimental rate $= k[\text{NO₂}][\text{F₂}]$. Proposed mechanism:
step 1 (slow) NO₂ + F₂ → NO₂F + F; step 2 (fast)
NO₂ + F → NO₂F.

1. Show that the steps sum to the overall equation.
   
2. Give the predicted rate law and say why.
   
3. Does the mechanism pass both tests?
   yes
4. Identify the intermediate.
   F
5. Does passing both tests prove the mechanism is correct? Explain.
   

**4.** `CED 5.8` For the mechanism A₂ → 2A (slow), then
A + B → AB (fast):

1. Overall equation:
   A₂ + 2B → 2AB
2. Predicted rate law: rate $= k[\text{A₂}]$
3. B is a reactant but absent from the rate law. Is that a problem?

**7.** `CED 5.6` Energy profiles.

1. $E_a(\text{fwd}) = 80\,\mathrm{kJ}$,
   $E_a(\text{rev}) = 120\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Find $\Delta H$ and
   classify. $-40$ kJ; exothermic
2. $E_a(\text{fwd}) = 150\,\mathrm{kJ}$,
   $\Delta H = +60\,\mathrm{kJ}$. Find $E_a(\text{rev})$.
   90 kJ
