> 📌 **Directions & data**
>
> $\Delta G^\circ = \Delta H^\circ - T\Delta S^\circ$ •\
> $\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K$ • $R = 8.314\,\mathrm{J/mol/K}$. ATP hydrolysis:
> $\Delta G^\circ = -30.5\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$. $S^\circ$ (J/K$\cdot$mol):
> H₂ 131 • O₂ 205 • H₂O(l) 70.
> $\Delta G_f^\circ$ (kJ/mol): H₂O(l) $-237$.

**1.** Dissolving a salt has three free-energy contributions. Name each
and give the sign of its enthalpy or entropy contribution:

1. Breaking up the crystal lattice:
   $\Delta H$ positive, $\Delta S$ positive
2. Reorganizing solvent around the ions:
   $\Delta S$ negative (hydration shells order)
3. Attraction between ions and solvent:
   $\Delta H$ negative

**2.** The CED says predicting the overall $\Delta G^\circ$ of
dissolution “can be challenging.” Explain why, and say what you
*are* expected to do.

**3.** NH₄NO₃ dissolves *endothermically* — the beaker gets
noticeably cold — yet it is extremely soluble.

1. What does the cold beaker tell you about $\Delta H^\circ$?
   it is positive
2. Since the salt dissolves readily, what must be true of
   $\Delta G^\circ$, and therefore of $\Delta S^\circ$?
   
3. Explain the entropy increase in physical terms.
   
4. What does this example prove about the claim “exothermic reactions
   are the favored ones”? 

**4.** NaCl dissolves with $\Delta H^\circ$ very close to zero, yet
readily. Explain what must be driving it and write the reduced form of the
free-energy expression that applies.

**5.** Two ways to drive an unfavorable process.

1. Name them. 
2. Give the CED's two examples of an external source.
   
3. What must two coupled reactions share for the coupling to be real
   rather than bookkeeping?
   a common intermediate

**6.** Coupled reaction arithmetic.

1. Reaction X has $\Delta G^\circ = +9.2$ kJ/mol and is coupled to ATP
   hydrolysis. Find the overall $\Delta G^\circ$ and say whether it is
   favored. $-21.3$ kJ/mol; favored
2. Reaction Y has $\Delta G^\circ = +50$ kJ/mol. Show that coupling to
   a single ATP hydrolysis is not enough, then find how many are
   needed.
   *(working space)*
3. Explain what “ATP is the cell's energy currency” actually means,
   thermodynamically. 

**7.** Photosynthesis converts CO₂ and water to glucose and oxygen
with $\Delta G^\circ \approx +2870$ kJ per mole of glucose.

1. Is the reaction thermodynamically favored?
   no
2. Explain how it occurs anyway, and why no law is broken.
   

**8.** **FRQ (10 points).** Consider the electrolysis of water,
2H₂O(l) → 2H₂(g) + O₂(g).

1. Predict the sign of $\Delta S^\circ$ and justify from the equation
   alone. 
2. Calculate $\Delta S^\circ$. 
   *(working space)*
3. Calculate $\Delta G^\circ$ at 298 K from
   $\Delta G_f^\circ$ values, and state whether the reaction is
   thermodynamically favored.
   *(working space)*
4. Given $\Delta H^\circ = +572$ kJ, calculate the temperature above
   which the reaction becomes favored, and comment on whether that is
   a practical route to hydrogen.
   *(working space)*
5. Explain how hydrogen is obtained from water in practice, in terms
   of this unit's vocabulary.
