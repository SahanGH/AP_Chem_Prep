0.50 mol O₂, and 0.20 mol Ar at

1. Total pressure: 2.46 atm
2. Mole fraction of O₂: 0.50
3. Partial pressure of O₂: 1.23 atm
4. If all the O₂ were removed, what happens to the partial
   pressure of N₂? 

**4.** State which KMT assumption each observation depends on:

1. Gas pressure rises when the container shrinks.
   constant random motion / collisions
2. All gases at the same $T$ have equal average kinetic energy.
   $KE \propto T$
3. Gas particles don't slow down over time in a sealed flask.
   elastic collisions

**5.** He and CO₂ are held at the same temperature.

1. Compare their average kinetic energies.
   equal — KE depends only on $T$
2. Compare their average speeds, with reasoning.
   
3. Sketch both Maxwell–Boltzmann curves on one set of axes and label
   them.
   *(working space)*

**6.** Under which conditions does a real gas behave most ideally,
and why? Name both failed assumptions in your answer.

**7.** For CH₄ at 200 K, a plot of $PV/nRT$ vs $P$
first dips below 1, then rises above 1.

1. Cause of the dip: attractions reduce collision
   force
2. Cause of the rise: particle volume becomes
   significant
3. Would He show a larger or smaller dip? Why?
