**4.** **Long FRQ (10 points).** A student determines the
concentration of a copper(II) solution by visible spectroscopy. Standards
are measured at 635 nm in a 1.00 cm cell:

| $c$ (M) | 0.0100 | 0.0200 | 0.0300 | 0.0400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $A$ | 0.125 | 0.250 | 0.375 | 0.500 |

1. On the axes below, plot $A$ versus $c$ and draw the best-fit line.
   *(working space)*
2. Determine the slope of the line, including units.
   
3. Calculate $\epsilon$, stating its units.
   
4. An unknown gives $A = 0.300$. Determine its concentration.
   
5. The student dilutes the unknown by half and re-measures. Predict the
   new absorbance and justify. 
6. Explain why the calibration must be performed at a single fixed
   wavelength.
