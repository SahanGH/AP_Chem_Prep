Unit 1 • Atomic Structure and Properties

Unit Exam

Mon Aug 24 • 48 minutes • periodic table provided

Section I • Multiple Choice 20 questions • 30 minutes • 1 point each

1. What is the mass of 0.50 mol of \(\ce{H2O}\) (\(M = 18\,\mathrm{g/mol}\))?
  1. 4.5 g
  2. 9.0 g
  3. 18 g
  4. 36 g
2. How many atoms are in 2.0 mol of neon?
  1. \(3.0\times10^{23}\)
  2. \(6.0\times10^{23}\)
  3. \(1.2\times10^{24}\)
  4. \(2.4\times10^{24}\)
3. Which sample contains the greatest number of atoms?
  1. 1.0 mol of \(\ce{NH3}\)
  2. 2.0 mol of He
  3. 1.0 mol of \(\ce{CO2}\)
  4. 3.0 mol of Ar
4. An element's mass spectrum shows peaks at 63 u and 65 u with relative heights of about 7 to 3. The average atomic mass is closest to
  1. 63.0 u
  2. 63.6 u
  3. 64.0 u
  4. 64.4 u
5. A mass spectrum of a pure element shows exactly three peaks. This is best explained by the element having three
  1. allotropes
  2. different charges
  3. naturally occurring isotopes
  4. electron shells
6. Boron's average atomic mass is 10.8 u, and its isotopes are \(\ce{^{10}B}\) and \(\ce{^{11}B}\). Which statement must be true?
  1. The two isotopes are equally abundant.
  2. \(\ce{^{11}B}\) is more abundant than \(\ce{^{10}B}\).
  3. \(\ce{^{10}B}\) is more abundant than \(\ce{^{11}B}\).
  4. Some boron atoms have a mass of exactly 10.8 u.
7. The empirical formula of \(\ce{C6H12O6}\) is
  1. \(\ce{C6H12O6}\)
  2. \(\ce{C3H6O3}\)
  3. \(\ce{CH2O}\)
  4. \(\ce{C2H4O2}\)
8. The percent by mass of oxygen in \(\ce{CO2}\) (\(M = 44\,\mathrm{g/mol}\)) is closest to
  1. 27%
  2. 50%
  3. 67%
  4. 73%
9. A compound is 75% carbon and 25% hydrogen by mass. Its empirical formula is
  1. \(\ce{CH}\)
  2. \(\ce{CH2}\)
  3. \(\ce{CH3}\)
  4. \(\ce{CH4}\)
10. Two compounds have the same empirical formula \(\ce{CH2}\) but different molar masses: 28 g/mol and 56 g/mol. Their molecular formulas are
  1. \(\ce{C2H4}\) and \(\ce{C4H8}\)
  2. \(\ce{CH2}\) and \(\ce{C2H4}\)
  3. \(\ce{C2H4}\) and \(\ce{C3H6}\)
  4. \(\ce{C2H2}\) and \(\ce{C4H4}\)
11. A particulate diagram shows some particles that are single filled circles and some that are filled–open pairs. The box represents
  1. a pure element
  2. a pure compound
  3. a mixture of an element and a compound
  4. a single diatomic element
12. The ground-state electron configuration of phosphorus is
  1. \([\ce{Ne}]\,3s^2\,3p^3\)
  2. \([\ce{Ne}]\,3s^2\,3p^4\)
  3. \([\ce{Ar}]\,3s^2\,3p^3\)
  4. \([\ce{Ne}]\,3s^1\,3p^4\)
13. Which configuration represents an excited state of a neutral nitrogen atom?
  1. \(1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^3\)
  2. \(1s^2\,2s^1\,2p^4\)
  3. \(1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^4\)
  4. \(1s^2\,2s^3\,2p^2\)
14. Which species has the same electron configuration as \(\ce{Ca^2+}\)?
  1. \(\ce{Na+}\)
  2. \(\ce{Ne}\)
  3. \(\ce{Cl-}\)
  4. \(\ce{Mg^2+}\)
15. In a photoelectron spectrum, the area of a peak indicates the
  1. binding energy of the electrons
  2. relative number of electrons in that subshell
  3. mass of the isotope
  4. energy of the incoming photons
16. A complete PES spectrum shows three peaks with relative areas 2 : 2 : 1, in order of decreasing binding energy. The element is
  1. lithium
  2. beryllium
  3. boron
  4. carbon
17. The 1s binding energy of Ne is 84 MJ/mol; that of Na is 104 MJ/mol. The best explanation is that Na has
  1. more protons pulling on essentially unshielded 1s electrons
  2. more electron shells than Ne
  3. a larger atomic radius than Ne
  4. fewer valence electrons than Ne
18. Which atom has the largest atomic radius?
  1. Na
  2. Mg
  3. Al
  4. Si
19. Successive ionization energies of an element (kJ/mol): 738, 1451, 7733, 10,540. The element is most likely in group
  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 13
  4. 14
20. Aluminum and oxygen form an ionic compound with the formula
  1. \(\ce{AlO}\)
  2. \(\ce{Al2O}\)
  3. \(\ce{AlO3}\)
  4. \(\ce{Al2O3}\)

Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 18 minutes

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

Silver's mass spectrum shows two peaks: 106.9  (51.8%) and 108.9  (48.2%).

  1. Calculate the average atomic mass of silver. Show your setup.
  2. Explain why the two peaks represent the same element even though the masses differ.
  3. A different sample of silver is found to be enriched in \(\ce{^{109}Ag}\). State how the calculated average atomic mass of this sample compares to your answer in (a), and why.

Free-response 2 • short • 4 points

The complete photoelectron spectrum of magnesium shows peaks at 126, 9.07, 5.31, and 0.74 MJ/mol with relative areas 2 : 2 : 6 : 2.

  1. Explain how the spectrum confirms the element is magnesium.
  2. Identify the peak associated with the electrons removed when Mg forms \(\ce{Mg^2+}\).
  3. Predict whether aluminum's 3s binding energy is greater or less than 0.74 MJ/mol, and justify with Coulombic reasoning.
