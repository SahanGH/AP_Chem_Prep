Unit 1 • Atomic Structure and Properties

Worksheet 1 • Mole & Formula Drill

Type A skill drill • CED 1.1, 1.3 • front = class, back = homework

Directions & data
Show the dimensional-analysis setup for every calculation. Correct sig figs and units required. No calculator on problems 1–4 — the numbers are chosen to be clean.
1. Mass of 2.0 mol of \(\ce{NaOH}\) (\(M = 40.0\,\mathrm{g/mol}\)): 80. g
2. Moles in 22 g of \(\ce{CO2}\): 0.50 mol
3. Molecules in 0.50 mol of \(\ce{H2O}\): \(3.0\times10^{23}\)
4. Total atoms in 1.0 mol of \(\ce{CaCl2}\) (formula units count 3 atoms each): \(1.8\times10^{24}\)
5. A tablet contains 500.0 mg of \(\ce{CaCO3}\) (\(M = 100.09\,\mathrm{g/mol}\)).
  1. Moles of \(\ce{CaCO3}\): \(4.996\times10^{-3}\) mol
  2. Moles of oxygen atoms: \(1.499\times10^{-2}\) mol
  3. Mass of calcium in the tablet:
    \(4.996\times10^{-3}\,\text{mol}\times 40.08\,\mathrm{g/mol}=0.2003\,\mathrm{g}\)
6. Percent by mass of nitrogen in \(\ce{NH4NO3}\) (\(M = 80.05\,\mathrm{g/mol}\)):
\(28.02/80.05\times100\%=35.00\%\)
7. A compound is 43.6% P and 56.4% O by mass.
  1. Empirical formula:
    P: \(43.6/30.97=1.41\); O: \(56.4/16.00=3.53\); \(3.53/1.41=2.5 \to \times2 \to\) \(\ce{P2O5}\)
  2. The molar mass is \(\approx284\,\mathrm{g/mol}\). Molecular formula: \(\ce{P4O10}\)
