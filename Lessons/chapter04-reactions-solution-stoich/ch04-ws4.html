Worksheet 4 • Redox & FRQ Practice

Zumdahl §4.9–4.11 • assign states first, conclusions second

Directions & data
Never name an oxidizing or reducing agent without first writing the oxidation states that justify it. Remember the label is counterintuitive: the species oxidized is the reducing agent.
1. Assign the oxidation state of the named element:
  1. S in \(\ce{SO3}\): \(+6\)
  2. N in \(\ce{NO2-}\): \(+3\)
  3. Cr in \(\ce{CrO4^2-}\): \(+6\)
  4. Cl in \(\ce{ClO3-}\): \(+5\)
  5. P in \(\ce{H3PO4}\): \(+5\)
  6. O in \(\ce{H2O2}\): \(-1\) (peroxide)
  7. H in \(\ce{NaH}\): \(-1\) (hydride)
  8. Fe in \(\ce{Fe2O3}\): \(+3\)
2. For each reaction: state whether it is redox; if so, identify what is oxidized, what is reduced, the oxidizing agent, and the reducing agent.
  1. \(\ce{Mg(s) + 2 HCl(aq) -> MgCl2(aq) + H2(g)}\)
  2. \(\ce{AgNO3(aq) + NaCl(aq) -> AgCl(s) + NaNO3(aq)}\)
  3. \(\ce{2 Na(s) + Cl2(g) -> 2 NaCl(s)}\)
3. Explain, in your own words, why the substance that is oxidized is called the reducing agent.
4. In \(\ce{Fe3O4}\), the four oxygens total \(-8\).
  1. What is the average oxidation state of iron? \(+8/3\)
  2. Explain why a non-integer value appears here.
5. FRQ (10 points). An iron ore sample is analyzed for iron content by redox titration. The dissolved iron is present as \(\ce{Fe^2+}\) and is titrated with standardized \(\ce{KMnO4}\): \[ \ce{5 Fe^2+(aq) + MnO4^-(aq) + 8 H+(aq) -> 5 Fe^3+(aq) + Mn^2+(aq) + 4 H2O(l)} \] A 1.250 g ore sample is dissolved and requires 31.20 mL of 0.0250 M \(\ce{KMnO4}\) to reach the endpoint.
  1. Determine the oxidation state of manganese in \(\ce{MnO4-}\) and in \(\ce{Mn^2+}\), and state whether Mn is oxidized or reduced.
  2. Identify the oxidizing agent and the reducing agent.
  3. Calculate the moles of \(\ce{MnO4-}\) used.
    \((0.03120)(0.0250) = 7.80e-4\,\mathrm{mol}\)
  4. Calculate the moles and mass of iron in the sample.
    \(5 \times 7.80\times10^{-4} = 3.90e-3\,\mathrm{mol}\) Fe; \(\times 55.85 = 0.2178\,\mathrm{g}\)
  5. Calculate the mass percent of iron in the ore. 17.4%
  6. Explain why no indicator is needed for this titration.
Rubric — score yourself, 10 points
(a) 2 pts 1 both oxidation states, 1 “reduced” with the decrease cited • (b) 2 pts 1 each agent, correctly matched to oxidized/reduced • (c) 1 pt \(7.80\times10^{-4}\) mol • (d) 2 pts 1 applies the 5:1 ratio, 1 mass with units • (e) 1 pt 17.4% • (f) 2 pts 1 names the colour change, 1 identifies it as self-indicating.
