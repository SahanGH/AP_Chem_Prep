Chapter 4 • Types of Chemical Reactions and Solution Stoichiometry

Chapter Test

Zumdahl §4.1–4.11 • 50 minutes • periodic table and solubility rules provided

Section I • Multiple Choice 20 questions • 30 minutes • 1 point each

1. Which substance is a weak electrolyte in aqueous solution?
  1. \(\ce{NaCl}\)
  2. \(\ce{HNO3}\)
  3. \(\ce{HC2H3O2}\)
  4. \(\ce{C6H12O6}\)
2. A 0.10 M solution of HA conducts electricity poorly, while 0.10 M HB conducts well. This shows that
  1. HB is more concentrated than HA
  2. HB ionizes to a greater extent than HA
  3. HA has a larger molar mass
  4. HA is not an acid
3. Water dissolves ionic solids primarily because water molecules
  1. are nonpolar and slip between ions
  2. are polar and form ion–dipole attractions with the ions
  3. react chemically with the ions
  4. have low molar mass
4. What is the concentration of nitrate ion in 0.20 M \(\ce{Ca(NO3)2}\)?
  1. 0.10 M
  2. 0.20 M
  3. 0.40 M
  4. 0.60 M
5. What volume of 6.00 M \(\ce{HCl}\) is needed to prepare 300. mL of 0.500 M \(\ce{HCl}\)?
  1. 12.5 mL
  2. 25.0 mL
  3. 50.0 mL
  4. 3.60 mL
6. Which compound is insoluble in water?
  1. \(\ce{KNO3}\)
  2. \(\ce{(NH4)2S}\)
  3. \(\ce{BaSO4}\)
  4. \(\ce{NaOH}\)
7. Mixing which pair produces a precipitate?
  1. \(\ce{NaCl(aq)}\) and \(\ce{KNO3(aq)}\)
  2. \(\ce{Pb(NO3)2(aq)}\) and \(\ce{KI(aq)}\)
  3. \(\ce{NaNO3(aq)}\) and \(\ce{NH4Cl(aq)}\)
  4. \(\ce{KCl(aq)}\) and \(\ce{Na2SO4(aq)}\)
8. Silver nitrate solution is added to sodium chloride solution. The white solid that forms is
  1. \(\ce{AgCl}\)
  2. \(\ce{NaNO3}\)
  3. \(\ce{Ag2O}\)
  4. \(\ce{NaCl}\)
9. Which ion makes essentially every compound it appears in soluble?
  1. \(\ce{NO3-}\)
  2. \(\ce{SO4^2-}\)
  3. \(\ce{CO3^2-}\)
  4. \(\ce{OH-}\)
10. In a complete ionic equation, which species is NOT written as separated ions?
  1. \(\ce{NaCl(aq)}\)
  2. \(\ce{HNO3(aq)}\)
  3. \(\ce{KOH(aq)}\)
  4. \(\ce{CaCO3(s)}\)
11. Ions present in solution both before and after a reaction are called
  1. limiting ions
  2. spectator ions
  3. complex ions
  4. polyatomic ions
12. The net ionic equation for mixing \(\ce{BaCl2(aq)}\) and \(\ce{Na2SO4(aq)}\) is
  1. \(\ce{BaCl2(aq) + Na2SO4(aq) -> BaSO4(s) + 2 NaCl(aq)}\)
  2. \(\ce{Ba^2+(aq) + SO4^2-(aq) -> BaSO4(s)}\)
  3. \(\ce{Na+(aq) + Cl^-(aq) -> NaCl(s)}\)
  4. \(\ce{Ba^2+(aq) + 2 Cl^-(aq) -> BaCl2(s)}\)
13. In a net ionic equation, aqueous acetic acid is written as \(\ce{HC2H3O2(aq)}\) rather than as ions because it is
  1. insoluble
  2. a nonelectrolyte
  3. a weak electrolyte
  4. a spectator
14. Which is a strong acid?
  1. \(\ce{HF}\)
  2. \(\ce{HClO4}\)
  3. \(\ce{HC2H3O2}\)
  4. \(\ce{H2CO3}\)
15. The net ionic equation for the reaction of \(\ce{HNO3(aq)}\) with \(\ce{NaOH(aq)}\) is
  1. \(\ce{H+(aq) + OH^-(aq) -> H2O(l)}\)
  2. \(\ce{HNO3(aq) + OH^-(aq) -> NO3^-(aq) + H2O(l)}\)
  3. \(\ce{Na+(aq) + NO3^-(aq) -> NaNO3(s)}\)
  4. \(\ce{H+(aq) + NaOH(aq) -> Na+(aq) + H2O(l)}\)
16. According to the Bronsted–Lowry definition, a base is a substance that
  1. produces hydroxide ions
  2. accepts a proton
  3. donates a proton
  4. conducts electricity
17. 25.0 mL of \(\ce{HCl}\) is neutralized by 50.0 mL of 0.100 M \(\ce{NaOH}\). The concentration of the \(\ce{HCl}\) is
  1. 0.0500 M
  2. 0.100 M
  3. 0.200 M
  4. 0.400 M
18. The oxidation state of chromium in \(\ce{Cr2O7^2-}\) is
  1. \(+3\)
  2. \(+7\)
  3. \(+6\)
  4. \(+12\)
19. In \(\ce{Zn(s) + Cu^2+(aq) -> Zn^2+(aq) + Cu(s)}\), zinc is
  1. oxidized and is the reducing agent
  2. oxidized and is the oxidizing agent
  3. reduced and is the reducing agent
  4. reduced and is the oxidizing agent
20. Which reaction is NOT an oxidation–reduction reaction?
  1. \(\ce{2 Mg + O2 -> 2 MgO}\)
  2. \(\ce{HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) -> NaCl(aq) + H2O(l)}\)
  3. \(\ce{Zn + 2 HCl -> ZnCl2 + H2}\)
  4. \(\ce{2 Na + Cl2 -> 2 NaCl}\)

Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 20 minutes

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

Aqueous solutions of barium hydroxide and sulfuric acid are mixed.

  1. Write the balanced formula equation for the reaction.
  2. Write the net ionic equation. Explain why, unusually, there are no spectator ions.
  3. Predict what happens to the electrical conductivity of the mixture as the reaction proceeds, and explain.

Free-response 2 • short • 4 points

A student mixes 50.0 mL of 0.200 M \(\ce{AgNO3}\) with 50.0 mL of 0.150 M \(\ce{K2CrO4}\). A red-brown precipitate of \(\ce{Ag2CrO4}\) (\(M = 331.7\,\mathrm{g/mol}\)) forms.

  1. Write the net ionic equation for the reaction.
  2. Determine the limiting reactant, showing the comparison.
  3. Calculate the mass of \(\ce{Ag2CrO4}\) produced.
