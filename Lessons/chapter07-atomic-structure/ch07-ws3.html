Chapter 7 • Atomic Structure and Periodicity

Worksheet 3 • Trends, Ionization Energy & PES

Zumdahl §7.12 • Coulombic reasons only — no octet language

Directions & data
Every trend explanation must use nuclear charge, distance, or shielding. “It wants a full shell” and “it is more stable” earn zero on the AP exam, so they earn zero here.
1. Rank each set and give the Coulombic reason:
  1. Atomic radius, largest first: Li, Na, K
  2. Atomic radius, largest first: Na, Mg, Al
  3. First ionization energy, largest first: F, O, N
2. Explain each period-2 anomaly:
  1. Why is \(I_1\) of boron less than that of beryllium?
  2. Why is \(I_1\) of oxygen less than that of nitrogen?
3. Successive ionization energies (kJ/mol). Identify the group and justify from the data:
  1. 496, 4562, 6910, 9543 \(\to\) group 1 — jump after \(I_1\)
  2. 786, 1577, 3232, 4356, 16,091 \(\to\) group 14 — jump after \(I_4\)
  3. 578, 1817, 2745, 11,577 \(\to\) group 13 — jump after \(I_3\) (Al)
4. Explain why \(I_2\) is always greater than \(I_1\), regardless of element.
5. Photoelectron spectroscopy. A spectrum shows peaks at 273, 26.8, 20.2, 2.44, and 1.25 MJ/mol with relative areas \(2:2:6:2:5\).
  1. Write the electron configuration. \(1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^5\)
  2. Identify the element, citing your evidence. Cl — areas total 17 electrons
  3. Which peak corresponds to the electrons removed first on ionization? 1.25 MJ/mol (3\(p\))
  4. Why does the 2\(p\) peak (20.2) lie at lower binding energy than the 2\(s\) peak (26.8), given both are in shell 2?
6. FRQ (10 points). Answer all parts.
  1. The complete PES spectrum of an element shows peaks at 208, 18.7, 13.5, 1.95, and 1.06 MJ/mol with relative areas \(2:2:6:2:3\). Determine the electron configuration and identify the element.
  2. Explain what the position and the area of a PES peak each represent.
  3. Predict two specific ways sulfur's spectrum would differ from this one.
  4. The 1\(s\) peak sits at 208 MJ/mol while the 3\(p\) peak sits at 1.06 — a factor of nearly 200. Explain this enormous gap.
  5. Successive ionization energies for this element are 1012, 1907, 2914, 4964, 6274, and 21,267 kJ/mol. Explain how these data independently confirm your identification in part (a).
Rubric — score yourself, 10 points
(a) 2 pts 1 configuration matching all five areas, 1 phosphorus tied to the 15-electron total • (b) 2 pts 1 position, 1 area • (c) 2 pts 1 shift to higher BE with the proton reason, 1 area change • (d) 2 pts 1 says 1\(s\) is unshielded/close, 1 says 3\(p\) is shielded and distant — “it's in a different shell” alone earns 1 • (e) 2 pts 1 locates the jump after \(I_5\), 1 links five valence electrons to group 15.
