Unit 2 • Compound Structure and Properties

Free-Response Set

CED 2.1–2.7 • 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Scope — two CED exclusions apply

Covers CED 2.1 through CED 2.7, worth 7 %–9 % of the exam.

Topic CED 2.3 excludes specific crystal structures, so unit-cell geometry is enrichment and is not asked. Topic CED 2.7 excludes hybridization involving \(d\) orbitals — for more than four electron domains only the shape is required, never an \(\ce{sp^3d}\) label — and also excludes molecular orbital theory. Neither appears below.

Every structural explanation must reach the Coulombic level. Rubrics award zero for “it wants a full octet”.

Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

Consider the thiocyanate ion, \(\ce{SCN-}\), whose skeleton is \(\ce{S-C-N}\).

  1. Determine the total number of valence electrons and draw a Lewis structure.
  2. Assign the formal charge on each atom in your structure.
  3. An alternative structure places a triple bond between C and N. State which structure is preferred and justify using formal charge.
  4. Predict the shape and bond angle about the central carbon, and state the model used.

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

  1. Bonding is better described as a continuum than as three separate categories. Place \(\ce{NaCl}\), \(\ce{HCl}\) and \(\ce{Cl2}\) on that continuum and justify using electronegativity.
  2. Sketch, in words, how the potential energy of two approaching atoms varies with internuclear distance, and identify what the minimum represents.
  3. The \(\ce{Cl2}\) bond energy is 243 kJ/mol and that of \(\ce{Br2}\) is 193 kJ/mol. Explain the difference.
  4. State how the potential-energy curve for \(\ce{Br2}\) differs from that for \(\ce{Cl2}\).

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

  1. Explain why ionic solids are hard and brittle, while being poor conductors as solids but good conductors when molten.

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

  1. Explain, using the electron sea model, why metals conduct electricity and can be drawn into wire.
  2. Steel is an alloy of iron with a small amount of carbon and is much harder than pure iron. Give the structural reason.

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

  1. State the hybridization of each carbon in ethanoic acid, \(\ce{CH3COOH}\).
  2. Count the sigma and pi bonds in the whole molecule.
