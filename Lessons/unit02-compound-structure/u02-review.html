The five tools

Bond classifier\(\Delta\)EN small + nonmetals \(\to\) covalent (polar if unequal); metal + nonmetal \(\to\) ionic; metal + metal \(\to\) metallic. It's a continuum — argue with \(\Delta\)EN
PE curveminimum's position \(=\) bond length; depth \(=\) bond energy; short-range wall \(=\) nuclear repulsion. Breaking bonds always costs energy
Lattice ranking\(|E| \propto |q_1q_2|/r\): charges first, radii as tiebreak
Lewis recipecount (charge-adjusted!) \(\to\) skeleton (least EN central, never H) \(\to\) outer octets \(\to\) leftovers on center \(\to\) multiple bonds if short. Check count AND octets
Shape chainLewis \(\to\) domains (multiple bond \(=\) 1) \(\to\) geometry \(\to\) angle (lone pairs compress) \(\to\) hybridization (\(2/3/4 =\) sp/sp\(^2\)/sp\(^3\)) \(\to\) polarity (dipoles + cancellation)

Formal charge and resonance in four lines

  • \(FC = \) valence \(-\) nonbonding \(- \frac12\)(bonding). Sum must equal the overall charge — always check.
  • Best structure: FCs near zero, negative FC on the most electronegative atom.
  • Resonance = one static average, never flipping. Evidence: equal bond lengths.
  • Bond order \(=\) bonding pairs \(\div\) positions: \(\ce{NO3-}\)/\(\ce{CO3^2-}\) \(= 4/3\); \(\ce{NO2-}\)/\(\ce{O3}\) \(= 3/2\).

The traps, one line each

  • Forgetting to add/subtract charge electrons in the count — kills the whole structure.
  • “Polar bonds, so polar molecule” — symmetry cancels (\(\ce{CO2}\), \(\ce{BF3}\), \(\ce{CCl4}\)).
  • Quoting 109.5\(^\circ\) for \(\ce{NH3}\)/\(\ce{H2O}\) — lone pairs compress (107\(^\circ\)/104.5\(^\circ\)).
  • Ranking lattices by size when charges differ — charges dominate.
  • Explaining brittleness or conductivity without naming the mobile (or immobile) charged particles.
  • Hybridization beyond sp\(^3\) — not assessed; don't burn time on it.
