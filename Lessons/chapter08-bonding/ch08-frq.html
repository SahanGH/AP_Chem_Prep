Chapter 8 • Bonding: General Concepts

Free-Response Set

Zumdahl §8.1–§8.13 • 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Scope

A heavily examined chapter: CED 2.1 (§8.1–8.3), 2.2 (§8.5, §8.8), 2.5 (Lewis diagrams, §8.9–8.11), 2.6 (resonance and formal charge, §8.12), 2.7 (VSEPR, §8.13), 1.8 (§8.4) and 6.7 (bond enthalpies, §8.8).

Throughout, explanations of trends must be Coulombic — charge, distance, shielding. Rubrics here award zero for answers that rest on “octet” or “stability” language alone.

Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

Consider the nitrate ion, \(\ce{NO3-}\).

  1. Draw a valid Lewis structure for \(\ce{NO3-}\), showing all lone pairs and the overall charge.
  2. Explain why \(\ce{NO3-}\) is described by three resonance structures, and state what the true structure is.
  3. State and justify the prediction for the three \(\ce{N-O}\) bond lengths.
  4. Predict the shape and the \(\ce{O-N-O}\) bond angle, and name the model used.

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

  1. Use the average bond enthalpies below to estimate \(\Delta H\) for \[ \ce{CH4(g) + 2O2(g) -> CO2(g) + 2H2O(g)} \] \(\ce{C-H}\) 413, \(\ce{O=O}\) 495, \(\ce{C=O}\) 799, \(\ce{O-H}\) 467 kJ/mol.
  2. Explain why this method gives only an estimate.
  3. Lithium fluoride and potassium fluoride are both ionic. Predict which has the larger lattice energy and justify in Coulombic terms.
  4. State the effect on lattice energy of replacing \(\ce{F-}\) with \(\ce{O^2-}\) in a compound of the same cation, and give the reason.

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

  1. \(\ce{CO2}\) and \(\ce{H2O}\) both contain polar bonds, yet only one molecule is polar. Identify which, and explain.
  2. State what determines whether a bond is polar.

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

For each species, state the number of electron domains on the central atom, the molecular shape, and the approximate bond angle.

  1. \(\ce{NH3}\)
  2. \(\ce{SO2}\)
  3. Explain why the bond angle in \(\ce{NH3}\) is smaller than the ideal tetrahedral angle.

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

The ions \(\ce{N^3-}\), \(\ce{O^2-}\), \(\ce{F-}\), \(\ce{Na+}\) and \(\ce{Mg^2+}\) all have ten electrons.

  1. Arrange them in order of increasing ionic radius.
  2. Justify the order.
