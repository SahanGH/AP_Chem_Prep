Chapter 8 • Bonding: General Concepts

Worksheet 3 • Structures, Resonance, VSEPR & FRQ

Zumdahl §8.9–8.13 • electron count first, always

Directions & data
Write the total valence electron count before drawing anything — an uncounted structure cannot be checked. Every geometry answer needs its domain count shown.
1. Electron counts and Lewis structures:
  1. \(\ce{NH4+}\)   count: 8
    N central, four N–H single bonds, no lone pairs, brackets with \(+\).
  2. \(\ce{SO3}\)   count: 24
    S central, three resonance forms with one S=O and two S–O (or all three drawn double); trigonal planar.
  3. \(\ce{XeF4}\)   count: 36
    Xe central with four Xe–F bonds and TWO lone pairs on Xe — expanded octet, 12 electrons around Xe.
2. Octet exceptions. Name the class and explain:
  1. \(\ce{NO2}\): odd-electron species (17 e\(^-\))
  2. \(\ce{BeCl2}\): fewer than an octet (Be gets 4)
  3. \(\ce{PCl5}\): expanded octet (period 3)
  4. Explain why \(\ce{NCl5}\) does not exist.
3. Formal charge:
  1. Compute FC on each atom in \(\ce{NO3-}\) for a form with one N=O and two N–O.
  2. State the two selection rules for choosing among candidate structures.
4. Resonance:
  1. Draw all resonance forms of \(\ce{CO3^2-}\) (24 e\(^-\)).
    Three forms with the C=O rotating among the three oxygens; brackets with \(2-\).
  2. Bond order of each C–O bond: \(4/3\)
  3. State the experimental evidence that carbonate is a hybrid rather than a molecule alternating between forms.
5. Complete the VSEPR chain for each species:
  1. \(\ce{PCl3}\): domains 4 (1 LP), geometry trigonal pyramidal, angle \(\approx107^\circ\)
  2. \(\ce{CS2}\): domains 2, geometry linear, angle 180\(^\circ\)
  3. \(\ce{H2S}\): domains 4 (2 LP), geometry bent, angle \(<109.5^\circ\)
  4. \(\ce{SO3}\): domains 3, geometry trigonal planar, angle 120\(^\circ\)
6. FRQ (10 points). Sulfur and carbon each form a triatomic oxide: \(\ce{SO2}\) and \(\ce{CO2}\).
  1. Give the total valence electron count for each.
  2. Draw a complete Lewis structure for each, showing all lone pairs.
    \(\ce{CO2}\): O=C=O, two lone pairs per O, none on C. \(\ce{SO2}\): bent, one S=O and one S–O (resonance), two lone pairs on each terminal O and ONE LONE PAIR ON S.
  3. State the electron-domain count, molecular geometry, and approximate bond angle for each.
  4. One is polar and one is not. Identify which and justify using bond dipoles and geometry.
  5. The two S–O bonds in \(\ce{SO2}\) are found to be equal in length. Explain.
Rubric — score yourself, 10 points
(a) 1 pt both counts • (b) 2 pts 1 \(\ce{CO2}\) correct; 1 \(\ce{SO2}\) correct including the lone pair on S — omitting it earns 0 for this point • (c) 3 pts 1 both domain counts, 1 both geometries, 1 both angles • (d) 2 pts 1 identifies \(\ce{SO2}\); 1 argues cancellation in both directions • (e) 2 pts 1 names resonance/hybrid, 1 links equal bond order to equal length — “the bond alternates” earns 0.
