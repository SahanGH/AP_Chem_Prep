Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 20 minutes

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

Consider \(\ce{2 H2(g) + O2(g) -> 2 H2O(g)}\).

  1. Calculate \(\Delta H\) using bond energies. Show which bonds are broken and which are formed.
  2. Explain why the reaction is exothermic, referring to the relative strengths of the bonds involved.
  3. The accepted value is -483.6 kJ. Explain the difference.

Free-response 2 • short • 4 points

Consider the compounds \(\ce{NaF}\), \(\ce{MgO}\), and \(\ce{KCl}\).

  1. Rank the three by lattice energy magnitude, largest first, and justify each comparison.
  2. \(\ce{MgO}\) melts near 2850 °C while \(\ce{NaF}\) melts near 990 °C. Explain the difference using Coulomb's law.
  3. Rank \(\ce{Na+}\), \(\ce{F-}\), and \(\ce{Mg^2+}\) by size and explain the principle you used.
