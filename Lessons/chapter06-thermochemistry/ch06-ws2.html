3. Verify Hess's law using Zumdahl's nitrogen oxides. Given \begin{align*} \ce{N2(g) + O2(g) &-> 2 NO(g)} &\Delta H &= 180\,\mathrm{kJ}\\ \ce{2 NO(g) + O2(g) &-> 2 NO2(g)} &\Delta H &= -112\,\mathrm{kJ} \end{align*}

Determine \(\Delta H\) for \(\ce{2 C(s) + H2(g) -> C2H2(g)}\) given \begin{align*} \text{(i)}\ \ce{C2H2(g) + 5/2 O2(g) &-> 2 CO2(g) + H2O(l)} &\Delta H &= -1300.\,\mathrm{kJ}\\ \text{(ii)}\ \ce{C(s) + O2(g) &-> CO2(g)} &\Delta H &= -393.5\,\mathrm{kJ}\\ \text{(iii)}\ \ce{H2(g) + 1/2 O2(g) &-> H2O(l)} &\Delta H &= -286\,\mathrm{kJ} \end{align*}
