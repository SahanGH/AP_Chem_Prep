Chapter 15 • Acid–Base Equilibria

Free-Response Set

Zumdahl §15.1–§15.6 • 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Scope

Every section of this chapter is examinable: CED 7.12 (common-ion effect, §15.1), 8.4 (§15.1–15.2), 8.5 (titrations, §15.4–15.6), 8.8 (properties of buffers, §15.2), 8.9 (Henderson–Hasselbalch, §15.2) and 8.10 (buffer capacity, §15.3).

Take \(K_a(\ce{CH3COOH}) = 1.8\times10^{-5}\), so \(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.74\), and \(K_w = 1.0\times10^{-14}\).

Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

A buffer is prepared containing 0.100 mol of \(\ce{CH3COOH}\) and 0.100 mol of \(\ce{NaCH3COO}\) in 1.00 L of solution.

  1. Calculate the pH of the buffer.
  2. 0.010 mol of \(\ce{HCl}\) is added, with no volume change. Calculate the new pH.
  3. Calculate the pH if 0.010 mol of \(\ce{NaOH}\) had been added to the original buffer instead.
  4. Adding 0.010 mol of \(\ce{HCl}\) to 1.00 L of pure water gives a pH of 2.00. Comment on the contrast with your answer to (b).

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

25.00 mL of 0.100 M \(\ce{CH3COOH}\) is titrated with 0.100 M \(\ce{NaOH}\).

  1. Calculate the initial pH, before any base is added.
  2. State the pH after 12.50 mL of base has been added, and justify without a full calculation.
  3. Calculate the pH at the equivalence point.
  4. Explain why the equivalence point is above pH 7, and state what this implies about choosing an indicator.

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

  1. Predict the effect on the pH of 0.100 M \(\ce{CH3COOH}\) of adding solid \(\ce{NaCH3COO}\), and justify.
  2. State what happens to the value of \(K_a\).

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

Two buffers both have pH 4.74. Buffer X contains 1.00 M acetic acid and 1.00 M acetate; buffer Y contains 0.010 M of each.

  1. State which resists pH change better, and why.
  2. Explain why both have the same starting pH despite this.

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

A titration curve for a monoprotic acid titrated with 0.100 M \(\ce{NaOH}\) shows a vertical rise centred at 20.0 mL, and the pH at 10.0 mL is 5.20.

  1. Determine the \(\mathrm{p}K_a\) and \(K_a\) of the acid.
  2. If the original sample was 25.0 mL, calculate the concentration of the acid.
