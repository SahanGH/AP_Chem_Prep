Chapter 16 • Solubility Equilibria

Free-Response Set

Zumdahl §16.1–§16.2 • 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Scope — re-verified against the 2024 CED

§16.1 is fully examinable: CED 7.11 (solubility equilibria), 7.12 (common-ion effect) and 8.11 (pH and solubility).

§16.2 is partially examinable. Predicting whether a precipitate forms by comparing \(Q\) with \(K_{sp}\) is supported by the framework and is examined in Question 2. Qualitative-analysis schemes are not.

§16.3 (complex ion equilibria) is off the framework entirely — the phrase “complex ion” appears nowhere in the CED — and no question uses it.

One exclusion shapes Question 2: under topic 8.11, pH and solubility is assessed qualitatively only. You will be asked to predict a direction and justify it, never to compute a solubility from a pH.

Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

Silver chromate, \(\ce{Ag2CrO4}\), has \(K_{sp} = 1.1\times10^{-12}\) at 25 °C.

  1. Write the dissolution equation and the \(K_{sp}\) expression.
  2. Calculate the molar solubility in pure water.
  3. Calculate the concentration of silver ion in that saturated solution.
  4. Predict, with justification, the effect on the molar solubility of dissolving the solid in 0.10 M \(\ce{AgNO3}\) instead of pure water.

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

Equal volumes of 2.0e-3 M \(\ce{AgNO3}\) and 2.0e-3 M \(\ce{Na2CrO4}\) are mixed.

  1. State the concentration of each ion immediately after mixing, before any reaction.
  2. Calculate \(Q\) and determine whether a precipitate of \(\ce{Ag2CrO4}\) forms.
  3. State the general rule relating \(Q\) and \(K_{sp}\) to what is observed.
  4. Calcium carbonate is far more soluble in acidic solution than in neutral water, but silver chloride is essentially unaffected by pH. Explain the difference. Do not calculate.

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

The molar solubility of \(\ce{BaSO4}\) in water at 25 °C is measured as 1.05e-5 M.

  1. Calculate \(K_{sp}\).
  2. Explain why this salt's expression is \(s^2\) while silver chromate's is \(4s^3\).

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

\(\ce{AgCl}\) has \(K_{sp} = 1.8\times10^{-10}\) and \(\ce{Ag2CrO4}\) has \(K_{sp} = 1.1\times10^{-12}\).
  1. A student concludes that \(\ce{Ag2CrO4}\) is less soluble because its \(K_{sp}\) is smaller. Evaluate this reasoning.

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

For each solid, predict whether its solubility increases, decreases or is essentially unchanged when the solution is acidified, and justify in one sentence.

  1. \(\ce{Mg(OH)2}\)
  2. \(\ce{CaF2}\)
  3. \(\ce{AgNO3}\)-derived \(\ce{AgBr}\)
