Chapter 18 • Electrochemistry

Worksheet 1 • Galvanic Cells and How They Work

Zumdahl §18.1 • An Ox, Red Cat — true in every cell there is

Directions & data
Never label an electrode positive or negative — the AP exam excludes it. Use anode and cathode. \(E^\circ\) (V): \(\ce{Ag+/Ag}\) \(+0.80\) • \(\ce{Cu^2+/Cu}\) \(+0.34\) •\ \(\ce{Pb^2+/Pb}\) \(-0.13\) • \(\ce{Fe^2+/Fe}\) \(-0.44\) •\ \(\ce{Zn^2+/Zn}\) \(-0.76\) • \(\ce{Mg^2+/Mg}\) \(-2.37\).
1. Name the component of a galvanic cell responsible for each function:
  1. Site of oxidation: the anode
  2. Site of reduction: the cathode
  3. Carries electrons between electrodes: the external wire
  4. Keeps each half-cell electrically neutral: the salt bridge
  5. Measures the driving force: the voltmeter
2. Explain in detail why a galvanic cell stops almost immediately if the salt bridge is removed.
3. Why does separating the two half-reactions produce useful energy when simply dropping zinc into copper(II) sulfate does not?
4. Consider \(\ce{Zn(s) + Cu^2+(aq) -> Zn^2+(aq) + Cu(s)}\) run as a galvanic cell.
  1. Write the two half-reactions and label each.
  2. Which electrode loses mass, and why?
  3. Which electrode gains mass, and why?
  4. In which direction do electrons flow through the wire? from zinc (anode) to copper (cathode)
  5. In which direction do anions move in the salt bridge? toward the anode
  6. What happens to \([\ce{Cu^2+}]\) as the cell runs? it decreases
5. Complete the comparison:
  1. Galvanic cells involve a reaction that is thermodynamically favored
  2. Electrolytic cells involve a reaction that is thermodynamically unfavored
  3. Galvanic cells have \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}}\) positive, electrolytic cells negative
  4. In both types, oxidation occurs at the anode and reduction at the cathode
  5. Which type requires an external power source? electrolytic
6. A student builds a cell from magnesium and copper. After an hour the magnesium strip is visibly thinner and the copper strip is coated.
  1. Identify the anode and cathode. anode Mg, cathode Cu
  2. Write the overall reaction. \(\ce{Mg + Cu^2+ -> Mg^2+ + Cu}\)
  3. Calculate \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}}\).
    \(E^\circ = 0.34 - (-2.37) = \mathbf{+2.71}~\text{V}\)
  4. Is the cell galvanic or electrolytic? Justify from your answer.
7. A student writes: “The anode is the negative electrode, so electrons flow away from it.” Explain why the second half of that sentence is fine but the first half should not appear on an AP answer.
8. Predict, with a calculation, whether each reaction occurs spontaneously as written:
  1. \(\ce{Pb(s) + 2Ag+(aq) -> Pb^2+(aq) + 2Ag(s)}\)
    \(E^\circ = 0.80 - (-0.13) = +0.93\) V — yes, favored
  2. \(\ce{Cu(s) + Zn^2+(aq) -> Cu^2+(aq) + Zn(s)}\)
    \(E^\circ = -0.76 - 0.34 = -1.10\) V — no, unfavored; this is the reverse Daniell cell
  3. \(\ce{Fe(s) + Cu^2+(aq) -> Fe^2+(aq) + Cu(s)}\) \(+0.78\) V; yes
Spiral review • Chapter 17 • spiral
  1. Favored means \(\Delta G^\circ\) is: negative
  2. Crossover temperature \(=\) \(\Delta H/\Delta S\)
  3. \(\Delta S^\circ\) sign when gas moles rise: positive
  4. Favored but very slow means: kinetic control
  5. \(S^\circ\) for an element is: not zero
