2. The overall reaction \(\ce{NO2 + CO -> NO + CO2}\) has the experimental rate law rate \(= k[\ce{NO2}]^2\). Consider the mechanism: \begin{align*} \ce{NO2 + NO2 &-> NO3 + NO} &&\text{slow}\\ \ce{NO3 + CO &-> NO2 + CO2} &&\text{fast} \end{align*}
  1. Show that the steps sum to the overall equation.
  2. Identify the intermediate.
  3. Show that the mechanism predicts the observed rate law.
  4. Explain why \(\ce{CO}\) does not appear in the rate law even though it is a reactant.
