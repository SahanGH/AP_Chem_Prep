Worksheet 1 • Rates and Rate Laws

Zumdahl §12.1–12.3 • orders come from data, never from coefficients

Directions & data
For every rate-law determination, show which two trials you compared and what was held constant. An order stated without that comparison cannot earn credit.
1. For \(\ce{2 N2O5 -> 4 NO2 + O2}\), write the rate of reaction in terms of each species.
2. For \(\ce{4 NH3 + 5 O2 -> 4 NO + 6 H2O}\), \(\ce{O2}\) disappears at 0.050 M/s.
  1. Rate of \(\ce{NH3}\) disappearance: 0.040 M/s
  2. Rate of \(\ce{H2O}\) formation: 0.060 M/s
  3. Rate of the reaction: 0.010 M/s
3. Explain the difference between average rate, instantaneous rate, and initial rate, and state why initial rates are used to determine rate laws.
4. For rate \(= k[\ce{A}][\ce{B}]^2\), state the effect on the rate of each change:
  1. Doubling [A]: doubles
  2. Doubling [B]: quadruples
  3. Doubling both: \(\times 8\)
  4. Halving [B]: quarters
  5. Overall order: 3
