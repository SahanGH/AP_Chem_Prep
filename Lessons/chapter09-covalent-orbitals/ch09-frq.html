Chapter 9 Covalent Bonding: Orbitals

Free-Response Set

Zumdahl §9.1 • 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Scope — most of this chapter is off the framework

Only §9.1 is examinable, as the orbital half of CED 2.7. Read the two exclusions before assigning:

1. Hybridization involving \(d\) orbitals is excluded. For a central atom with more than four electron domains the CED requires the shape only. \(\ce{sp^3d}\) and \(\ce{sp^3d^2}\) labels are not assessed and are not asked for here. 2. Molecular orbital theory is excluded. §9.2–9.5 — MO diagrams, bond order from MO theory, paramagnetism of \(\ce{O2}\) — are enrichment. They are genuinely illuminating and they will not be on the exam. No question below uses them.

What remains is worth doing properly: hybridization of second-row central atoms, and the sigma/pi count that explains why double bonds cannot rotate.

Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

Consider the molecules \(\ce{CH4}\), \(\ce{C2H4}\) (ethene) and \(\ce{C2H2}\) (ethyne).

  1. State the number of electron domains around each carbon atom and the hybridization that follows.
  2. State the bond angle and geometry about carbon in each.
  3. Explain the rule that connects the number of domains to the hybridization.
  4. Count the sigma and pi bonds in ethene, \(\ce{C2H4}\).

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

  1. Describe how a sigma bond and a pi bond differ in the way their orbitals overlap.
  2. Explain why rotation about the \(\ce{C=C}\) bond in ethene is restricted, while rotation about the \(\ce{C-C}\) bond in ethane is essentially free.
  3. State one observable chemical consequence of that restricted rotation.
  4. State the hybridization of the nitrogen atom in \(\ce{NH3}\), and explain why the lone pair must be counted.

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

State the hybridization of the central atom in each species.

  1. \(\ce{BF3}\)
  2. \(\ce{H2O}\)
  3. \(\ce{CO2}\)

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

  1. State the number of sigma and pi bonds in \(\ce{C2H2}\) (ethyne).
  2. State the number of sigma and pi bonds in \(\ce{HCN}\).

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

Sulfur hexafluoride, \(\ce{SF6}\), has six electron domains around sulfur.

  1. State the molecular shape and the \(\ce{F-S-F}\) bond angle.
  2. Explain why six domains adopt this arrangement.
