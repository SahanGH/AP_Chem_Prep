Chapter 9 • Covalent Bonding: Orbitals

Worksheet 2 • FRQ Practice $+$ Optional Enrichment

Zumdahl §9.1 examinable • §9.2–9.3 optional

Directions & data
Problems 1–2 are exam-relevant. Problem 3 is marked OPTIONAL — molecular orbital theory is not on the AP exam, and skipping it costs you nothing.
1. FRQ (10 points). Consider the molecule \(\ce{CH3CH=CH2}\) (propene) and the ion \(\ce{NO2-}\).
  1. Give the hybridization of each of the three carbon atoms in propene, with the domain count that justifies each.
  2. State the total number of \(\sigma\) and \(\pi\) bonds in propene.
  3. Explain why rotation is free about the \(\ce{CH3-CH}\) bond but restricted about the \(\ce{CH=CH2}\) bond.
  4. For \(\ce{NO2-}\) (18 valence electrons), give the electron-domain count, the molecular geometry, and the hybridization of nitrogen.
Rubric — score yourself, 10 points
(a) 3 pts 1 per carbon, each requiring the domain count as justification — bare labels earn half • (b) 2 pts 1 \(\sigma\) count, 1 \(\pi\) count • (c) 3 pts 1 names \(\sigma\) symmetry, 1 names the \(\pi\) requirement for parallel \(p\) orbitals, 1 concludes that rotating breaks the bond • (d) 2 pts 1 domain count with the lone pair identified, 1 geometry AND sp\(^2\).
2. Assign hybridization and predict the bond angle:
  1. P in \(\ce{PH3}\): sp\(^3\), \(\approx107^\circ\)
  2. C in \(\ce{HCN}\): sp, \(180^\circ\)
  3. S in \(\ce{SO3}\): sp\(^2\), \(120^\circ\)
  4. O in \(\ce{CH3OH}\): sp\(^3\), \(\approx104.5^\circ\)
3. OPTIONAL — enrichment only. Molecular orbital theory is not assessed on the AP exam. Attempt this only if you worked Block 3.
  1. Give the formula for bond order in MO theory. (bonding \(-\) antibonding) \(\div\) 2
  2. \(\ce{He2}\) would have 2 bonding and 2 antibonding electrons. Compute the bond order and explain what it means physically.
  3. The Lewis structure of \(\ce{O2}\) shows all electrons paired, yet liquid oxygen is attracted to a magnet. Explain the discrepancy and how MO theory resolves it.
  4. State one thing each model does better than the other.
Spiral review • Chapter 9 • block 1–2
  1. Hybridization of C in \(\ce{CO2}\): sp
  2. \(\sigma\) and \(\pi\) in \(\ce{C2H4}\): 5 \(\sigma\), 1 \(\pi\)
  3. Domains on N in \(\ce{NH3}\): 4
  4. Which bond type blocks rotation? \(\pi\)
