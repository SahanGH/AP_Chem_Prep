Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 32 minutes

Data

rate \(= k[\ce{A}]^m[\ce{B}]^n\) •\ \(\ln[\ce{A}] = -kt + \ln[\ce{A}]_0\) •\ \(1/[\ce{A}] = kt + 1/[\ce{A}]_0\) •\ \(t_{1/2} = 0.693/k\) (first order) •\ \(k = Ae^{-E_a/RT}\) •\ \(\ln(k_2/k_1) = -\tfrac{E_a}{R}(\tfrac{1}{T_2}-\tfrac{1}{T_1})\) • \(R = 8.314\,\mathrm{J/mol/K}\).

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

A reaction is followed and three plots are prepared. Only the plot of \(1/[\ce{A}]\) against time is linear; it has slope \(+0.42\) and intercept \(5.0\).

  1. State the order of the reaction and justify from the graphs.
  2. Give \(k\) with units and \([\ce{A}]_0\).
  3. Calculate the half-life at the start of the reaction.

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

  3. Predict the initial rate when \([\ce{NO}] = 0.030\) M and \([\ce{O2}] = 0.015\) M.
