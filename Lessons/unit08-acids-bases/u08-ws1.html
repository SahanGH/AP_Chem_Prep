Unit 8 • Acids and Bases

Worksheet 1 • Conjugates, pH, and Strong Acids

CED 8.1–8.2 • neutral means [H+] = [OH-], not pH 7

Directions & data
\(\mathrm{pH} = -\log[\ce{H+}]\) •\ \(\mathrm{pH} + \mathrm{pOH} = 14.00\) •\ \(K_aK_b = K_w = 1.0\times10^{-14}\) • six strong acids: \(\ce{HCl}\), \(\ce{HBr}\), \(\ce{HI}\), \(\ce{HNO3}\), \(\ce{H2SO4}\), \(\ce{HClO4}\).
1. CED 8.1 Identify the conjugate:
  1. Conjugate base of \(\ce{H2SO4}\): \(\ce{HSO4^-}\)
  2. Conjugate acid of \(\ce{HCO3^-}\): \(\ce{H2CO3}\)
  3. Conjugate base of \(\ce{HCO3^-}\): \(\ce{CO3^2-}\)
  4. Conjugate acid of \(\ce{NH3}\): \(\ce{NH4+}\)
2. CED 8.1 For \(\ce{HNO2(aq) + H2O(l) <=> H3O+(aq) + NO2^-(aq)}\), label all four species.
3. CED 8.1 Explain why water can act as a Brønsted–Lowry base, referring to its structure.
4. CED 8.1 Conjugate strength.
  1. The stronger the acid, the weaker its conjugate base.
  2. \(K_a \times K_b\) equals \(K_w\)
  3. Given \(K_a(\ce{HC2H3O2}) = 1.8\times10^{-5}\), find \(K_b\) for acetate. \(5.6\times10^{-10}\)
  4. Given \(K_b(\ce{NH3}) = 1.8\times10^{-5}\), find \(K_a\) for \(\ce{NH4+}\). \(5.6\times10^{-10}\)
  5. Rank as bases, weakest first: \(\ce{Cl-}\), \(\ce{F-}\), \(\ce{CN-}\).
5. CED 8.2 pH and pOH conversions at 25 °C:
  1. \([\ce{H+}] = 2.5\times10^{-3}\): pH \(=\) 2.60
  2. pH \(= 8.40\): \([\ce{H+}] =\) \(4.0\times10^{-9}\)
  3. \([\ce{OH-}] = 5.0\times10^{-5}\): pH \(=\) 9.70
  4. pH \(= 11.20\): pOH \(=\) 2.80
  5. pH 3 is how many times more acidic than pH 6? 1000
6. CED 8.2 Explain why pure water at 50 °C has a pH below 7 yet is still neutral.
7. CED 8.2 Strong acid and base pH:
  1. 0.020 M \(\ce{HCl}\): 1.70
  2. 0.0010 M \(\ce{HNO3}\): 3.00
  3. 0.025 M \(\ce{NaOH}\): 12.40
  4. 0.015 M \(\ce{Ba(OH)2}\):
    \([\ce{OH-}] = 2(0.015) = 0.030\); pOH \(= 1.52\); pH \(= \mathbf{12.48}\)
  5. 0.0050 M \(\ce{Ca(OH)2}\): 12.00
8. CED 8.2 A student calculates the pH of 0.010 M \(\ce{Sr(OH)2}\) as 12.00. Identify the error and give the correct value.
9. CED 8.2 Name the six strong acids, and say which common acid is conspicuously absent from the list.
