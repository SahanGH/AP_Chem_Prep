Unit 8 • Acids and Bases

Worksheet 2 • Weak Acids and Weak Bases

CED 8.3 • for a base, x is [OH-] — convert

Directions & data
\(K_a\): \(\ce{HF}\) \(7.2\times10^{-4}\) • \(\ce{HC2H3O2}\) \(1.8\times10^{-5}\) • \(\ce{HOCl}\) \(3.5\times10^{-8}\) •\ \(\ce{HCN}\) \(6.2\times10^{-10}\). \(K_b(\ce{NH3}) = 1.8\times10^{-5}\). \(K_w = 1.0\times10^{-14}\). Always run the 5% check.
1. CED 8.3 Set up the ICE table for 0.250 M \(\ce{HOCl}\) and write \(K_a\) in terms of \(x\).
2. CED 8.3 Calculate the pH of each weak acid solution, with the 5% check:
  1. 0.100 M \(\ce{HC2H3O2}\):
    \(x = \sqrt{(1.8\times10^{-5})(0.100)} = 1.34\times10^{-3}\); pH \(= \mathbf{2.87}\). Check: 1.3% ✓
  2. 0.500 M \(\ce{HOCl}\):
    \(x = \sqrt{(3.5\times10^{-8})(0.500)} = 1.32\times10^{-4}\); pH \(= \mathbf{3.88}\). Check: 0.026% ✓
  3. 0.0500 M \(\ce{HCN}\): pH \(= 5.25\)
3. CED 8.3 0.10 M \(\ce{HCl}\) has pH 1.00; 0.10 M acetic acid has pH 2.87. Explain the difference and state what it shows about the words “strong” and “concentrated.”
4. CED 8.3 Percent ionization.
  1. For 0.100 M acetic acid: 1.3%
  2. For 0.0100 M acetic acid, \([\ce{H+}] = 4.24\times10^{-4}\). Percent ionization? 4.24%
  3. The dilute solution has the higher percent ionization but the higher pH. Explain.
5. CED 8.3 A 0.20 M solution of a weak acid has pH 3.10.
  1. \([\ce{H+}] = \) \(7.9\times10^{-4}\)
  2. Calculate \(K_a\).
    \(K_a \approx (7.9\times10^{-4})^2/0.20 = \mathbf{3.1\times10^{-6}}\)
  3. p\(K_a = \) 5.51
6. CED 8.3 Calculate the pH of each weak base solution:
  1. 0.150 M \(\ce{NH3}\):
    \(x = \sqrt{(1.8\times10^{-5})(0.150)} = 1.64\times10^{-3} = [\ce{OH-}]\); pOH \(= 2.78\); pH \(= \mathbf{11.22}\)
  2. 0.400 M \(\ce{NH3}\): pH \(= 11.43\)
  3. 0.100 M \(\ce{NH3}\): pH \(= 11.13\)
7. CED 8.3 A student calculates \(x = 1.64\times10^{-3}\) for 0.150 M \(\ce{NH3}\) and reports pH \(= 2.78\). Identify the error and give the correct answer.
8. CED 8.3 Polyprotic acids. \(\ce{H3PO4}\) has \(K_{a1} = 7.5\times10^{-3}\), \(K_{a2} = 6.2\times10^{-8}\), \(K_{a3} = 4.8\times10^{-13}\).
  1. Why is each successive constant so much smaller?
  2. Justify calculating the pH from \(K_{a1}\) alone.
  3. Which polyprotic acid is the exception, and why?
