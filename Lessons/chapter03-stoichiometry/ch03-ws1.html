Chapter 3 • Stoichiometry

Worksheet 1 • Moles and Formulas

Zumdahl §3.1–3.7 • every answer needs units and correct sig figs

Directions & data
Show the dimensional-analysis setup, not just the number. An answer with no units is wrong even when the digits are right.
1. Molar masses — build each from the periodic table:
  1. \(\ce{Mg(OH)2}\): 58.33 g/mol
  2. \(\ce{Al2(SO4)3}\): 342.17 g/mol
  3. \(\ce{C6H12O6}\): 180.16 g/mol
2. Conversions:
  1. Moles in 54.0 g \(\ce{H2O}\): 3.00 mol
  2. Mass of 0.250 mol \(\ce{CaCO3}\): 25.0 g
  3. Molecules in 11.0 g \(\ce{CO2}\): \(1.50\times10^{23}\)
  4. Oxygen atoms in 0.100 mol \(\ce{Al2(SO4)3}\): \(7.23\times10^{23}\)
3. A sample of an unknown element has a mass of 4.00 g and contains \(9.03\times10^{22}\) atoms.
  1. Moles present: 0.150 mol
  2. Molar mass: 26.7 g/mol
  3. Most likely identity: aluminum (26.98)
4. Percent composition:
  1. \(\%\,\ce{N}\) in \(\ce{NH4NO3}\): 35.00%
  2. \(\%\,\ce{O}\) in \(\ce{Al2(SO4)3}\): 56.11%
  3. Mass of nitrogen in 50.0 g of \(\ce{NH4NO3}\): 17.5 g
5. A compound is 26.6% K, 35.4% Cr, and 38.1% O by mass.
  1. Empirical formula:
    K: \(26.6/39.10 = 0.680\); Cr: \(35.4/52.00 = 0.681\); O: \(38.1/16.00 = 2.38\). Divide by 0.680: \(1 : 1 : 3.5 \to \times2 \to\) \(\ce{K2Cr2O7}\)
  2. Why must the 3.5 be doubled rather than rounded to 4?
6. A hydrocarbon is 82.7% C and 17.3% H by mass, with a molar mass of 58.12 g/mol.
  1. Empirical formula: \(\ce{C2H5}\)
  2. Molecular formula: \(\ce{C4H10}\)
7. Combustion analysis. A 0.2000 g sample of a compound containing only C, H, and O yields 0.4547 g \(\ce{CO2}\) and 0.1862 g \(\ce{H2O}\). Carry four significant figures throughout — combustion data is unforgiving of early rounding.
  1. Mass of C: 0.1241 g
  2. Mass of H: 0.02083 g
  3. Mass of O (by difference): 0.0551 g
  4. Empirical formula:
    C: \(0.01033\); H: \(0.02066\); O: \(0.003444\) mol. Divide by 0.003444: C 3.00, H 6.00, O 1.00 \(\to\) \(\ce{C3H6O}\).
  5. If the molar mass is 58.08 g/mol, give the molecular formula. \(\ce{C3H6O}\) (same)
