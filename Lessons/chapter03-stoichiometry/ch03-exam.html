Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 20 minutes

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

A compound contains only carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen. Analysis shows it is 54.5% C and 9.10% H by mass, and its molar mass is 88.11 g/mol.

  1. Determine the mass percent of oxygen.
  2. Determine the empirical formula. Show your mole work.
  3. Determine the molecular formula.

Free-response 2 • short • 4 points

Aluminum reacts with chlorine: \(\ce{2 Al + 3 Cl2 -> 2 AlCl3}\). A mixture of 27.0 g Al and 142 g \(\ce{Cl2}\) is allowed to react to completion.

  1. Determine the limiting reactant, showing the comparison that justifies your choice.
  2. Calculate the theoretical yield of \(\ce{AlCl3}\) in grams.
  3. If 112 g of \(\ce{AlCl3}\) is actually isolated, calculate the percent yield.
  4. State one reason a percent yield is typically less than 100%.
