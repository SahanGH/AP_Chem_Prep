Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

A compound found in living cells contains only carbon, hydrogen and oxygen. Analysis gives 40.00 % C, 6.71 % H and 53.29 % O by mass. Its molar mass is 180.2 g/mol.

  1. Determine the empirical formula.
  2. Determine the molecular formula.
  3. Calculate the number of moles in a 25.0 g sample.
  4. Calculate the number of oxygen atoms in that 25.0 g sample.
  5. Explain why percent composition alone cannot distinguish this compound from formaldehyde, \(\ce{CH2O}\).

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

The thermite reaction produces molten iron: \[ \ce{Al(s) + Fe2O3(s) -> Al2O3(s) + Fe(l)} \quad \text{(unbalanced)} \] A mixture of 25.0 g of aluminium and 85.0 g of iron(III) oxide is ignited.

  1. Balance the equation.
  2. Identify the limiting reactant, showing your reasoning.
  3. Calculate the theoretical yield of iron in grams.
  4. The reaction actually produced 45.9 g of iron. Calculate the percent yield.
  5. Give one reason, other than experimental spillage, why the percent yield is below 100 %.

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

Copper has two stable isotopes. A mass spectrum gives \(\ce{^{63}Cu}\), 62.930 u, abundance 69.17 %; and \(\ce{^{65}Cu}\), 64.928 u, abundance 30.83 %.

  1. Calculate the average atomic mass of copper.
  2. State which peak in the spectrum is taller, and why.
  3. Explain why the tabulated atomic mass of copper is not a whole number.

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

  1. Calculate the molar mass of calcium nitrate, \(\ce{Ca(NO3)2}\).
  2. Calculate the number of nitrate ions in 45.0 g of \(\ce{Ca(NO3)2}\).

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

  1. Write the balanced equation for the complete combustion of propane, \(\ce{C3H8}\), in oxygen.
  2. A student balances an equation by changing \(\ce{H2O}\) to \(\ce{H2O2}\). Explain why this is not permitted.
