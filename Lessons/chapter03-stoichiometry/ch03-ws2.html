Chapter 3 • Stoichiometry

Worksheet 2 • Balancing and Mass Relationships

Zumdahl §3.8–3.10 • the road always runs through moles

Directions & data
Balance before you calculate — an unbalanced equation makes every downstream number wrong. Never change a subscript to balance.
1. Balance each equation:
  1. \(\ce{Na + H2O -> NaOH + H2}\) \(\ce{2 Na + 2 H2O -> 2 NaOH + H2}\)
  2. \(\ce{C4H10 + O2 -> CO2 + H2O}\) \(\ce{2 C4H10 + 13 O2 -> 8 CO2 + 10 H2O}\)
  3. \(\ce{Fe2O3 + CO -> Fe + CO2}\) \(\ce{Fe2O3 + 3 CO -> 2 Fe + 3 CO2}\)
  4. \(\ce{Ca3(PO4)2 + H2SO4 -> CaSO4 + H3PO4}\) \(\ce{Ca3(PO4)2 + 3 H2SO4 -> 3 CaSO4 + 2 H3PO4}\)
2. A student "balances" \(\ce{H2 + O2 -> H2O}\) by writing \(\ce{H2 + O2 -> H2O2}\). Explain what is wrong in one sentence, then balance it correctly.
3. For \(\ce{2 KClO3 -> 2 KCl + 3 O2}\):
  1. Moles of \(\ce{O2}\) from 4.0 mol \(\ce{KClO3}\): 6.0 mol
  2. Moles of \(\ce{KClO3}\) needed for 1.5 mol \(\ce{O2}\): 1.0 mol
  3. Mass of \(\ce{O2}\) from 24.5 g \(\ce{KClO3}\) (\(M = 122.55\)):
    \(24.5/122.55 = 0.200\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\ce{KClO3}\) \(\times \tfrac32 = 0.300\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\ce{O2}\) \(\times 32.00 = 9.60\,\mathrm{g}\)
4. Ammonia burns: \(\ce{4 NH3 + 5 O2 -> 4 NO + 6 H2O}\). Starting with 34.0 g \(\ce{NH3}\) and excess oxygen:
  1. Moles of \(\ce{NH3}\): 2.00 mol
  2. Mass of NO produced:
    \(2.00\) mol \(\ce{NH3}\) \(\to 2.00\) mol NO \(\times 30.01 = 60.0\,\mathrm{g}\)
  3. Mass of \(\ce{O2}\) consumed:
    \(2.00 \times \tfrac54 = 2.50\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\ce{O2}\) \(\times 32.00 = 80.0\,\mathrm{g}\)
  4. Mass of water produced, then verify conservation of mass across the whole reaction.
    \(2.00\times\tfrac64 = 3.00\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\ce{H2O}\) \(\times 18.02 = 54.1\,\mathrm{g}\). Check: \(34.0+80.0 = 114.0\) in; \(60.0+54.1 = 114.1\) out (rounding).
5. Thermite: \(\ce{Fe2O3 + 2 Al -> 2 Fe + Al2O3}\). What mass of aluminum is required to produce 100.0 g of iron?
\(100.0/55.85 = 1.791\,\mathrm{mol}\) Fe; Al:Fe is \(2{:}2 = 1{:}1 \to 1.791\,\mathrm{mol}\) Al \(\times 26.98 = 48.3\,\mathrm{g}\)
6. Explain, in one or two sentences, why the coefficients of a balanced equation give mole ratios but not mass ratios.
