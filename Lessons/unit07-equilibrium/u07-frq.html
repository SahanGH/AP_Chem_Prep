Unit 7 • Equilibrium

Free-Response Set

CED 7.1–7.12 • 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Scope — one CED exclusion applies

Covers CED 7.1 through CED 7.12, worth 7 %–9 % of the exam. Note that CED 7.11 and CED 7.12 reach forward into solubility equilibria (Zumdahl §16.1), which is why \(K_{sp}\) appears in an equilibrium unit.

Topic CED 7.3 excludes converting between \(K_c\) and \(K_p\). The conceptual difference between them is examinable; the \(K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}\) conversion is not, and is not asked below.

Section II • Free Response 5 questions • 32 points • 50 minutes

Free-response 1 • long • 10 points

At a certain temperature \(K_c = 0.0211\) for \[ \ce{PCl5(g) <=> PCl3(g) + Cl2(g)} \] A vessel is charged with 0.500 M \(\ce{PCl5}\) and nothing else.

  1. Write the equilibrium expression and construct an ICE table.
  2. Show that the approximation \(0.500 - x \approx 0.500\) is not valid here.
  3. Calculate the equilibrium concentration of each species.
  4. The volume of the vessel is doubled at constant temperature. State the direction of the shift and justify.

Free-response 2 • long • 10 points

  1. For the same reaction (\(K_c = 0.0211\)), a mixture is found to contain \([\ce{PCl5}] = 0.10\,\mathrm{M}\), \([\ce{PCl3}] = 0.20\,\mathrm{M}\) and \([\ce{Cl2}] = 0.20\,\mathrm{M}\). Determine the direction of net change.
  2. State what the magnitude of \(K_c = 0.0211\) tells you about the equilibrium mixture.
  3. A student claims that because \(K\) is small, the reaction must be slow. Evaluate.
  4. Calcium fluoride has \(K_{sp} = 3.9e-11\). Calculate its molar solubility in pure water, then state and justify the effect of dissolving it in 0.10 M \(\ce{NaF}\) instead.

Free-response 3 • short • 4 points

  1. Write the equilibrium expression for \(\ce{NH4HS(s) <=> NH3(g) + H2S(g)}\).
  2. Adding more solid \(\ce{NH4HS}\) to the sealed container does not change the pressure of \(\ce{NH3}\). Explain.

Free-response 4 • short • 4 points

  1. A sealed flask holds \(\ce{H2O(l)}\) in equilibrium with \(\ce{H2O(g)}\). A few molecules of the liquid are replaced with water containing the heavy isotope \(\ce{^{18}O}\). After some time the heavy isotope is found in both phases. Explain what this shows.
  2. State what would be observed macroscopically during this time.

Free-response 5 • short • 4 points

For the exothermic equilibrium \(\ce{2SO2(g) + O2(g) <=> 2SO3(g)}\), \(\Delta H < 0\):

  1. State and justify the effect of raising the temperature on the amount of \(\ce{SO3}\) and on the value of \(K\).
  2. State the effect of adding a catalyst on the amount of \(\ce{SO3}\).
