  4. constant composition

  4. Elements are composed of atoms.

Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 17 minutes

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

Two oxides of nitrogen are analyzed. In oxide A, 1.00 g of N is combined with 1.14 g of O. In oxide B, 1.00 g of N is combined with 2.28 g of O.

  1. Calculate the ratio of the masses of oxygen combined with a fixed mass of nitrogen, and state which law the result illustrates.
  2. Explain why this result supports the existence of atoms rather than a continuous model of matter.
  3. If oxide A is \(\ce{NO}\), give the formula of oxide B.

Free-response 2 • short • 4 points

Consider the ion \(\ce{^{52}_{24}Cr^{3+}}\) and the compounds it forms.

  1. State the number of protons, neutrons, and electrons in this ion.
  2. Write the formula of the compound this ion forms with the oxide ion, and name it.
  3. Chromium also forms \(\ce{CrO}\). Name it and explain why a Roman numeral is required for chromium but not for calcium.
