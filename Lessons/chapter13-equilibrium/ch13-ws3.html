Chapter 13 • Chemical Equilibrium

Worksheet 3 • Le Ch^atelier's Principle & FRQ

Zumdahl §13.7 • only temperature changes the value of $K$

Directions & data
For every prediction, state the direction of shift AND whether the value of \(K\) changes. Those are two separate questions and both are examinable.
1. For \(\ce{2 SO2(g) + O2(g) <=> 2 SO3(g)}\), \(\Delta H = -198\,\mathrm{kJ}\), predict the shift and state whether \(K\) changes:
  1. Add \(\ce{SO2}\): right; \(K\) unchanged
  2. Remove \(\ce{SO3}\): right; \(K\) unchanged
  3. Decrease the volume: right (3 mol \(\to\) 2 mol); \(K\) unchanged
  4. Increase the temperature: left; \(K\) decreases
  5. Add a catalyst: no shift; \(K\) unchanged
  6. Add helium at constant volume: no shift; \(K\) unchanged
2. Explain each of the three "no shift" cases above:
  1. Why does a catalyst cause no shift?
  2. Why does adding an inert gas at constant volume cause no shift?
  3. Why does adding more of a pure solid cause no shift?
3. Only one kind of change alters the numerical value of \(K\).
  1. Name it. temperature
  2. Explain why the others do not.
  3. For an endothermic reaction, does raising the temperature increase or decrease \(K\)? Explain using heat as a reactant.
4. Zumdahl's arsenic roasting reaction: \(\ce{As4O6(s) + 6 C(s) <=> As4(g) + 6 CO(g)}\). Predict the effect of each change:
  1. Adding \(\ce{CO}\): shifts left
  2. Adding more carbon: no shift (pure solid)
  3. Removing \(\ce{As4}\) gas as it forms: shifts right
  4. Increasing the container volume: shifts right (more gas moles)
5. FRQ (10 points). The Haber process synthesizes ammonia: \[ \ce{N2(g) + 3 H2(g) <=> 2 NH3(g)} \qquad \Delta H = -92\,\mathrm{kJ} \]
  1. Write the \(K_c\) expression, and determine \(\Delta n\) and the relationship between \(K_p\) and \(K_c\).
  2. A reaction vessel at equilibrium contains \([\ce{N2}] = 0.50\,\mathrm{M}\), \([\ce{H2}] = 1.0\,\mathrm{M}\), and \([\ce{NH3}] = 0.20\,\mathrm{M}\). Calculate \(K_c\).
    \(K_c = (0.20)^2/[(0.50)(1.0)^3] = 0.040/0.50 = 0.080\)
  3. Some \(\ce{NH3}\) is then removed. State the direction of shift, and state whether \(K_c\) changes.
  4. Predict and explain the effect on the equilibrium yield of \(\ce{NH3}\) of (i) increasing the pressure and (ii) increasing the temperature.
  5. Industrial plants nevertheless operate at roughly 450 °C. Explain this apparent contradiction.
Rubric — score yourself, 10 points
(a) 2 pts 1 \(K_c\) expression, 1 \(\Delta n\) with the \(K_p\) relation • (b) 1 pt 0.080 • (c) 2 pts 1 direction, 1 explicitly states \(K\) unchanged •\ (d) 3 pts 1 pressure with the mole-count reason, 1 temperature direction, 1 states that \(K\) itself decreases •\ (e) 2 pts 1 identifies the rate problem, 1 names the trade-off against yield.
