Chapter 14 • Acids and Bases

Chapter Test

Zumdahl §14.1–14.9 • 50 minutes • $K$ values provided with Section II

Scope

Covers §14.1–14.9. Nothing from §14.10 (oxides) or §14.11 (the Lewis model) appears — both were marked enrichment in the notes, and the Lewis model is not on the AP framework.

  1. a substance that produces \(\ce{OH-}\) in water

  1. \(\ce{H3PO4}\)
  2. \(\ce{HPO4^2-}\)
  3. \(\ce{PO4^3-}\)

  1. \(\ce{Cl-}\)

  1. stronger its conjugate base
  2. weaker its conjugate base

  4. lower its concentration

  1. \(1.0\times10^{-7}\)
  2. \(1.0\times10^{-14}\)
  3. \(1.0\times10^{7}\)

9. A solution with pH 3 is how many times more acidic than one with pH 6?
  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 30
  4. 1000

  2. neutral, because \([\ce{H+}] = [\ce{OH-}]\)

  4. impossible

  1. 4.20

  1. 1.00

  4. 0.10 M \(\ce{HCN}\)

Section II • Free Response 2 questions • 20 minutes

Data
\(K_a\): \(\ce{HF}\) \(7.2\times10^{-4}\) • \(\ce{HC2H3O2}\) \(1.8\times10^{-5}\) • \(\ce{HOCl}\) \(3.5\times10^{-8}\) •\ \(\ce{HCN}\) \(6.2\times10^{-10}\). \(K_b\): \(\ce{NH3}\) \(1.8\times10^{-5}\). \(K_w = 1.0\times10^{-14}\).

Free-response 1 • short • 4 points

A 0.250 M solution of acetic acid is prepared.

  1. Write the ionization equation and the \(K_a\) expression.
  2. Calculate the pH, showing the ICE setup.
  3. Verify that the approximation used was valid.

Free-response 2 • short • 4 points

Sodium cyanide, \(\ce{NaCN}\), is dissolved in water to give a 0.100 M solution.

  1. Predict whether the solution is acidic, basic, or neutral, and justify by considering both ions.
  2. Write the hydrolysis equation and calculate \(K_b\) for \(\ce{CN-}\).
  3. Calculate the pH of the solution.
