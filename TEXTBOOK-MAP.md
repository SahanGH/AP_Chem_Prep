## The headline: nothing is missing, but almost nothing is where the CED puts it

I checked the four topics I'd flagged as textbook gaps against the actual text. **All four are covered.** Corrected:

| CED Topic | Earlier claim | Reality |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **1.6 Photoelectron Spectroscopy** | "Essentially absent" | **Wrong — fully covered.** §7.12, PDF pp. 368–369: the $E_{\text{electron}} = h\nu - KE$ treatment, He(58.4 nm/21.2 eV) source, Fig. 7.33 idealized P spectrum, B/F overlay comparison. This is the AP-edition addition |
| **1.2 Mass Spectra** | "Thin" | **Wrong — properly covered.** §3.2, PDF pp. 112–115: Fig. 3.2 neon spectrum, Fig. 3.3 copper worked example computing average atomic mass from peak heights |
| **3.13 Beer–Lambert** | "Thin" | **Covered, but buried in Appendix 3** (PDF pp. 1142–1144), with spectrophotometer schematic Fig. A.6. Students will never find it unless you assign it explicitly |
| **3.9 Separation of Mixtures** | "Barely covered" | **Covered in §1.10** (PDF pp. 53–57): distillation, filtration, chromatography with mobile/stationary phase treatment |

**The real problem is dislocation, not absence.** Unit 3 — your heaviest at 18–22% — pulls from Chapters 1, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11, *and* Appendix 3. Seven locations. A student told to "read Chapter 10" gets a fraction of the unit.

**Consequence for the build:** far less original content than v3 assumed. Instead, every unit map carries a **precise page-range reading list**, and the notes fill only the genuine connective gaps. That is a meaningfully cheaper build.

## Where original content is still needed

Small and specific, now that the map is accurate:

1. **Particulate-level representations** throughout. Zumdahl predates how heavily AP leans on draw-and-interpret-the-particles items. Every unit's Type B worksheets carry this.
2. **PES interpretation practice.** The exposition (pp. 368–369) is solid; there are few practice items. Original problem sets needed.
3. **Beer–Lambert problem sets.** Appendix 3 explains it; no end-of-chapter exercises attach to it.
4. **Connective tissue for Unit 3.** Seven scattered sources need one coherent narrative. This is the largest single writing job in the course.
5. **AP-style FRQ items** across all units — Zumdahl's exercises are not in AP FRQ format.
